# DZShop — Login Help

## Overview

This guide helps you log in to your DZShop account and troubleshoot login issues.

## How to Log In

### Method 1: Website

1. Go to `dzshop.dz`.
2. Click **"Log In"** (top-right corner).
3. Enter your **email address** or **phone number**.
4. Enter your **password**.
5. Click **"Log In"**.

### Method 2: Mobile App

1. Open the DZShop app.
2. Tap **"Log In"**.
3. Enter your email/phone and password.
4. Tap **"Log In"**.

### Method 3: Social Login

DZShop supports login with:

- **Google Account** — Click "Continue with Google".
- **Facebook Account** — Click "Continue with Facebook".

> ⚠️ Social login is only available if you previously linked your social account to DZShop.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

If you enabled 2FA, after entering your password:

1. You'll be asked for a **6-digit code**.
2. The code is sent by:
   - **SMS** (to your registered phone number), OR
   - **Authenticator App** (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, etc.)
3. Enter the code within 5 minutes.
4. Click **"Verify"**.

### Lost 2FA Device?

If you lost your phone or can't access your authenticator app:

1. Click **"Lost your device?"** on the 2FA page.
2. You'll be redirected to a recovery process.
3. You'll need to verify your identity via:
   - Email verification.
   - Phone call to your registered number.
   - ID document upload (in extreme cases).
4. Our team will review within 24 hours.

## Login Issues

### Issue 1: "Invalid email or password"

**Possible causes:**
- Wrong email/phone number.
- Wrong password (typo, caps lock).
- Account was deleted.

**Solutions:**
1. Check your email/phone for typos.
2. Make sure Caps Lock is off.
3. Try the **"Forgot Password"** feature.
4. If you forgot your email, try your phone number instead.
5. If the issue persists, contact support.

### Issue 2: "Account locked"

After **5 failed login attempts**, your account is locked for **30 minutes**.

**Solutions:**
1. Wait 30 minutes and try again.
2. Use **"Forgot Password"** to reset your password (this also unlocks the account).
3. Contact support at 3030 if urgent.

### Issue 3: "Account not verified"

If you created an account but didn't verify your email:

**Solutions:**
1. Check your email for the verification link.
2. Check spam/junk folder.
3. Request a new verification email from the login page.
4. If still no email, contact support@dzshop.dz.

### Issue 4: "Account suspended"

Accounts can be suspended for:
- Suspected fraud.
- Multiple payment disputes.
- Abusive behavior toward customer service.
- Violation of Terms and Conditions.

**What to do:**
- You'll receive an email explaining the suspension.
- To appeal, email `security@dzshop.dz` within 7 days.
- Our team will review your case within 5 business days.

### Issue 5: "Cannot log in with Google/Facebook"

**Possible causes:**
- You didn't link this social account to DZShop.
- Your social account email differs from your DZShop email.
- Browser cookies are blocked.

**Solutions:**
1. Try logging in with email and password instead.
2. Check your browser cookie settings.
3. Try a different browser.
4. Link your social account in **My Account** → **Settings** → **Linked Accounts**.

### Issue 6: "Logged out unexpectedly"

**Possible causes:**
- You logged in from another device (max 3 devices).
- Session expired (after 30 days of inactivity).
- Security logout (after a security event).
- Password was changed.

**Solutions:**
1. Log in again.
2. If you didn't change your password, contact security@dzshop.dz.

### Issue 7: "Cannot access account from new device"

If 2FA is enabled, you'll need to verify the new device:

1. Log in with email and password.
2. Enter the 2FA code sent to your phone.
3. Verify the new device by clicking the link in the verification email.

## Staying Logged In

When logging in, you can check **"Remember me"**:

- Keeps you logged in for **30 days** (website) or **90 days** (app).
- Do **NOT** use this on shared/public computers.

## Logging Out

### Website
- Click your name (top-right) → **"Log Out"**.

### Mobile App
- Tap **Menu** → **Settings** → **Log Out**.

> 💡 Always log out from shared or public computers.

## Login from Multiple Devices

You can be logged in on up to **3 devices** simultaneously:

- 1 phone
- 1 tablet
- 1 computer

If you try to log in on a 4th device, the oldest session is automatically logged out.

## Account Takeover Protection

If we detect suspicious login activity (login from a new country, multiple failed attempts, unusual order patterns):

1. We may temporarily lock your account.
2. We'll send an email and SMS notification.
3. You'll need to verify your identity before logging in again.
4. If you didn't initiate the activity, contact `security@dzshop.dz` immediately.
