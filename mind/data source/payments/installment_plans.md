# DZShop — Installment Plans

## Overview

DZShop offers **0% interest installment plans** for orders above 10,000 DZD. This allows customers to spread the cost of their purchase over several months.

## Available Plans

| Plan | Number of Payments | Order Range (DZD) | Eligible Cards |
|------|-------------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **3x** | 3 monthly payments | 10,000 – 100,000 | CIB, Edahabia |
| **6x** | 6 monthly payments | 30,000 – 300,000 | CIB, Edahabia |
| **12x** | 12 monthly payments | 50,000 – 500,000 | CIB only |

## How Installments Work

### Example: 3x Plan
You order a product for **30,000 DZD** and choose 3x installments:

| Payment | Amount | Date |
|---------|--------|------|
| 1st payment (today) | 10,000 DZD | Order date |
| 2nd payment | 10,000 DZD | +1 month |
| 3rd payment | 10,000 DZD | +2 months |
| **Total** | **30,000 DZD** | (0% interest) |

### Example: 6x Plan
You order a laptop for **120,000 DZD** and choose 6x installments:

| Payment | Amount | Date |
|---------|--------|------|
| 1st payment (today) | 20,000 DZD | Order date |
| 2nd payment | 20,000 DZD | +1 month |
| 3rd payment | 20,000 DZD | +2 months |
| 4th payment | 20,000 DZD | +3 months |
| 5th payment | 20,000 DZD | +4 months |
| 6th payment | 20,000 DZD | +5 months |
| **Total** | **120,000 DZD** | (0% interest) |

### Example: 12x Plan
You order a TV for **180,000 DZD** and choose 12x installments (CIB only):

| Payment | Amount | Date |
|---------|--------|------|
| 1st payment (today) | 15,000 DZD | Order date |
| Monthly payments | 15,000 DZD | +1, +2, ... +11 months |
| **Total** | **180,000 DZD** | (0% interest) |

## Eligibility

### Customer Eligibility

To use installments, you must:

1. Be **18 years or older**.
2. Have a **DZShop account** (verified email and phone).
3. Have a **CIB card** (for all plans) or **Edahabia** (for 3x and 6x only).
4. Have a **valid Algerian ID** (we may verify).
5. Have **no outstanding installment debt** with DZShop.
6. Have placed at least **1 previous order** with DZShop (paid in full).

> ⚠️ First-time customers cannot use installments. Place at least one order with full payment first.

### Card Eligibility

| Card Type | 3x | 6x | 12x |
|-----------|----|----|-----|
| CIB Debit | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Edahabia | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| BaridiMob | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| DZShop Wallet | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |

### Product Eligibility

Most products are eligible for installments, except:

- ❌ Products on sale or clearance (already discounted).
- ❌ Products under 10,000 DZD (below minimum).
- ❌ Digital products (eBooks, gift cards).
- ❌ Groceries (pilot category).

## How to Use Installments at Checkout

1. Add products to your cart.
2. Proceed to checkout.
3. Select your delivery address and shipping option.
4. In the **Payment Method** section, select **"Pay in Installments"**.
5. Choose the plan:
   - 3x (if order is 10,000–100,000 DZD)
   - 6x (if order is 30,000–300,000 DZD)
   - 12x (if order is 50,000–500,000 DZD and you have a CIB card)
6. Enter your card details.
7. The **first installment** is charged immediately.
8. Review and confirm the order.
9. You'll see a payment schedule in the confirmation email.

## Payment Schedule

- **1st payment**: Charged immediately at checkout.
- **Subsequent payments**: Charged automatically on the **same date** each month.
- **Example**: If you ordered on March 15, future payments are on April 15, May 15, etc.

### What if the Payment Date Falls on a Weekend or Holiday?

- The payment is processed on the **next business day**.
- Example: Payment date is Sunday → processed on Monday.

### Failed Automatic Payment

If your card has insufficient funds on the payment date:

1. We'll attempt the charge again on day 2.
2. If it fails again on day 3, we'll attempt on day 5.
3. If it fails on day 5, your installment plan is in **default**.

## Late Payment

If an installment payment is late:

| Days Late | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| 1–7 days | Email + SMS reminder |
| 8–14 days | Phone call from our team |
| 15–30 days | Account suspended (no new orders) |
| 30+ days | Sent to collection agency |

## Fees

- ✅ **Interest**: 0% (no interest charges).
- ✅ **Setup fee**: Free.
- ⚠️ **Late payment fee**: 500 DZD per late installment.
- ⚠️ **Card rejection fee**: 200 DZD per failed automatic charge.

## Early Repayment

You can pay off your installments early:

1. Go to **My Account** → **My Installments**.
2. Select the installment plan.
3. Click **"Pay Off Early"**.
4. Enter your card details.
5. The remaining balance is charged.
6. No early repayment fees.

## Cancelling an Installment Order

You can cancel an installment order:

### Before delivery (within 2 hours):
- Full cancellation.
- First installment is refunded (10–15 business days).
- No fees.

### After delivery (within 7 days):
- Treat as a return (see `returns/return_policy.md`).
- All paid installments are refunded.
- Future installments are cancelled.
- Refund processing: 10–15 business days.

### After 7 days:
- Cannot cancel.
- Must continue paying installments.
- Or return the product (if eligible).

## Tracking Your Installments

You can view your installment plans:

1. **Website**: My Account → My Installments.
2. **App**: Menu → My Installments.
3. **Email**: Monthly statements are sent by email.

### Installment Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | Plan is in progress, payments on schedule |
| **Completed** | All installments paid |
| **Late** | One or more payments are late |
| **Default** | 30+ days late, sent to collection |
| **Cancelled** | Plan cancelled (with refund if eligible) |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### "Can I have multiple installment plans at the same time?"

Yes, up to **3 active plans** maximum. The total outstanding balance cannot exceed 500,000 DZD.

### "Can I change my card for future installments?"

Yes:
1. My Account → My Installments → Select Plan → Change Card.
2. Enter new card details.
3. Future installments will use the new card.

### "What if my card expires during the plan?"

You'll receive an email 30 days before expiration:
1. Update your card details in My Account.
2. If not updated, payments will fail.
3. Late fees may apply.
