# DZShop — Track Your Order

## Overview

All DZShop orders come with **free tracking**. Once your order is shipped, you can track its location and expected delivery time.

## When Does Tracking Become Available?

| Order Status | Tracking Available? |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Order Placed | ❌ No (order is being processed) |
| Order Confirmed | ❌ No (preparing for shipment) |
| Order Shipped | ✅ Yes (tracking link is activated) |
| Out for Delivery | ✅ Yes (live update) |
| Delivered | ✅ Final status |
| Returned | ✅ Final status |

> ⏱️ Tracking usually becomes active within **2–4 hours** after the order is shipped (depending on the shipping partner).

## How to Track Your Order

### Method 1: DZShop Website

1. Go to `dzshop.dz/track`.
2. Enter your **order number** (e.g., DZ-2025-0012345).
3. Enter your **phone number** (last 4 digits) for verification.
4. Click **"Track Order"**.
5. You'll see the current status, location, and estimated delivery date.

### Method 2: DZShop Mobile App

1. Open the DZShop app.
2. Log in to your account.
3. Go to **"My Orders"**.
4. Select the order you want to track.
5. The tracking information will be displayed automatically.

### Method 3: Email/SMS Tracking Link

When your order is shipped, you receive:

- **Email** with a "Track Your Order" button.
- **SMS** with a tracking link.

Click the link to open the tracking page directly (no login required).

### Method 4: Phone (Hotline)

1. Call **3030** (free from any Algerian mobile).
2. Press **1** for order tracking.
3. Enter your order number using the keypad.
4. Listen to the automated status update.

### Method 5: WhatsApp

1. Send a WhatsApp message to **+213 770 12 34 56**.
2. Send your order number (e.g., DZ-2025-0012345).
3. You'll receive an automated reply with the current status.
4. If you need more help, a customer service agent will assist you (during working hours).

### Method 6: Shipping Partner's Website

You can also track directly on the shipping partner's website:

| Partner | Tracking Website |
|---------|------------------|
| Yalidine | `yalidine.express/tracking` |
| Maystro | `maystro-delivery.dz/tracking` |
| Algérie Télécom | `epresso.poste.dz` |
| DZShop Express | Tracking link in email/SMS |

You'll need the **tracking number** (different from your order number), which is sent in the shipment confirmation email.

## Order Statuses Explained

### 1. Order Placed
- You've successfully placed the order.
- We're verifying your payment (if applicable).
- **Time in this status:** 5–30 minutes (instant for online payments, 24–72 hours for bank transfers).

### 2. Order Confirmed
- Payment is verified.
- Our team is preparing your order for shipment.
- Products are being picked, packed, and labeled.
- **Time in this status:** 2–24 hours (depends on stock and order volume).

### 3. Order Shipped
- Your order has left our warehouse.
- The shipping partner has picked it up.
- Tracking is now active.
- **Time in this status:** Until delivery (depends on shipping option).

### 4. Out for Delivery
- The package is on the delivery vehicle.
- It will be delivered today.
- **Time in this status:** 2–8 hours.

### 5. Delivered
- The package has been delivered to:
  - You (or someone at your address)
  - The Stop Desk you selected
  - A neighbor (only if you authorized this in delivery instructions)
- **Final status.**

### 6. Delivery Failed
- The delivery agent attempted delivery but couldn't reach you.
- A second attempt will be made the next day.
- After 3 failed attempts, the order is returned to a Stop Desk (7 days to pick up).

### 7. Returned to Sender
- The order was not picked up within 7 days.
- It's being returned to DZShop.
- A refund will be processed (5–10 business days).

## Estimated Delivery Time

The estimated delivery time depends on:

1. **Shipping option** chosen at checkout.
2. **Destination wilaya**.
3. **Product availability** (pre-order items take longer).
4. **Order placement time** (orders after 14:00 may be processed the next day).

Typical delivery times:

| Shipping Option | Algiers | Other Major Cities | Remote Areas |

## Tracking Issues

### "My tracking hasn't updated in 24 hours"

Possible reasons:
- The shipping partner's system has a delay.
- The package is in transit between cities (no scan points).
- Weekend or holiday (no updates).

**What to do:**
- Wait 24 more hours.
- If still no update after 48 hours, contact us.

### "The tracking shows 'Delivered' but I didn't receive anything"

Possible reasons:
- The package was delivered to a neighbor.
- The package was left at the Stop Desk (check your SMS for pickup code).
- The delivery agent marked it as delivered by mistake.

**What to do:**
1. Check with your neighbors.
2. Check your SMS for any pickup notifications.
3. If you still can't find the package, contact us within **48 hours** at:
   - Hotline 3030
   - WhatsApp +213 770 12 34 56
   - Email: support@dzshop.dz

### "The tracking link doesn't work"

- Try copying the link into a different browser.
- Try the alternative tracking methods (website, app, hotline).
- Contact us if the issue persists.

## Delivery Notifications

You'll receive notifications at each step:

1. **Order Placed** — Email + SMS
2. **Order Confirmed** — Email + SMS
3. **Order Shipped** — Email + SMS (with tracking link)
4. **Out for Delivery** — SMS
5. **Delivered** — Email + SMS
6. **Delivery Failed** — SMS (with new attempt date)

> 💡 Make sure your phone number and email are correct in your account to receive all notifications.
