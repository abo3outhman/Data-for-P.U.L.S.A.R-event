# DZShop — Loyalty Program (DZShop Points)

## Overview

The **DZShop Points** loyalty program rewards customers for every purchase. Points can be redeemed for discounts, free products, and exclusive benefits.

## How It Works

### Earning Points

For every **100 DZD** spent on DZShop, you earn **1 DZShop Point**.

| Action | Points Earned |
|--------|---------------|
| Place an order | 1 point per 100 DZD spent |
| Write a product review | 10 points (after approval) |
| Refer a friend (first order) | 100 points |
| Birthday bonus | 200 points |
| Subscribe to newsletter | 50 points (one-time) |
| Follow on social media | 25 points per platform (max 100) |
| Complete your profile | 50 points (one-time) |
| Year-end bonus (VIP only) | 500 points |

### Example Calculation

| Order Amount | Points Earned |
|--------------|---------------|
| 1,000 DZD | 10 points |
| 5,000 DZD | 50 points |
| 10,000 DZD | 100 points |
| 50,000 DZD | 500 points |
| 100,000 DZD | 1,000 points |

### Points Not Earned On

- ❌ Shipping fees.
- ❌ COD handling fees (200 DZD).
- ❌ Returns and refunds (points are deducted for returned items).
- ❌ Gift card purchases.
- ❌ Wallet top-ups.

## Redeeming Points

### Discount Redemption

You can redeem points for discounts at checkout:

| Points Redeemed | Discount Value |
|-----------------|----------------|
| 100 points | 50 DZD off |
| 500 points | 250 DZD off |
| 1,000 points | 500 DZD off |
| 2,500 points | 1,250 DZD off |
| 5,000 points | 2,500 DZD off |
| 10,000 points | 5,000 DZD off |

### How to Redeem

1. At checkout, find the **"Redeem Points"** section.
2. Select the number of points to redeem.
3. The discount is applied to your order total.
4. Points are deducted from your account.

### Free Product Redemption

VIP members can redeem points for free products:

| Points Required | Product Examples |
|------------------|-------------------|
| 5,000 points | T-shirt, phone case, book |
| 10,000 points | Power bank, headphones |
| 25,000 points | Smartwatch (basic), Bluetooth speaker |

To redeem for a free product:
1. Go to **My Account** → **Rewards** → **Free Products**.
2. Browse available products.
3. Select a product.
4. Confirm redemption.
5. The product is shipped to your address (free shipping).

## Points Expiration

| Tier | Expiration Policy |
|------|-------------------|
| Standard | Points expire after **24 months** of inactivity |
| VIP | Points **never expire** (as long as VIP status is maintained) |

> ⚠️ "Inactivity" means no purchases for 24 months. Any purchase resets the expiration timer.

## Points History

You can view your points history:

1. **Website**: My Account → My Points → History.
2. **App**: Menu → My Points → History.

History includes:
- Date.
- Action (earned, redeemed, expired).
- Amount.
- Order number (if applicable).

## Referral Program

### How It Works

1. Get your referral code: **My Account** → **Refer Friends**.
2. Share your code with friends.
3. Your friend enters the code at signup.
4. When your friend places their first order:
   - Your friend gets: **500 DZD discount** on first order.
   - You get: **100 DZShop Points**.

### Rules

- Referral code must be entered at signup (cannot be added later).
- Your friend must be a new customer (no previous account).
- Your friend's first order must be above 3,000 DZD.
- Maximum referrals per year: **50**.
- Self-referral is not allowed.
- Referral codes cannot be sold.

## Birthday Bonus

### Standard Members
- **200 points** added to your account on your birthday.
- **10% discount** coupon valid for 30 days from your birthday.

### VIP Members
- **500 points** added to your account.
- **Free product** (worth ~2,000 DZD) shipped to your address.
- **20% discount** coupon valid for 30 days.

> To receive birthday bonuses, set your date of birth in your profile (at least 30 days before your birthday).

## Year-End Bonus (VIP Only)

At the end of each calendar year, VIP members receive:

- **500 points** (added December 31).
- **Special VIP-only coupon** (15% off, valid January).
- **Thank you email** with year-in-review stats.

## Maintaining VIP Status

### How to Reach VIP
- Accumulate **5,000 points** (lifetime, not annual).
- Once you reach 5,000 points, you become VIP permanently (as long as your account is active).

### How to Lose VIP Status
- Account deletion.
- Account suspension for fraud.
- Inactivity for 36 months (no purchases).
- Voluntary downgrade (rare).

> VIP status is **not lost** if you redeem all your points. It's based on lifetime points earned.

## Points Transfer

- ❌ Points cannot be transferred between accounts.
- ❌ Points cannot be sold.
- ❌ Points cannot be converted to cash.
- ✅ Points can be redeemed for discounts or products only.

## Points and Returns

When you return a product:
- Points earned for that product are **deducted** from your account.
- If you used points for a discount on the returned order:
  - The points are **refunded** to your account.
  - Refund processing: 24 hours.

### Example
- Order: 10,000 DZD → earned 100 points.
- Used 500 points for 250 DZD discount.
- Paid: 9,750 DZD.
- Return the entire order:
  - Refund: 9,750 DZD (5–10 business days).
  - 100 points deducted (earned on this order).
  - 500 points refunded (used on this order).
  - Net points change: +400 points.

## Common Issues

### "My points are not showing"

- Wait 24 hours (sometimes delay).
- Refresh your account page.
- Check if the order is "Completed" (not "Pending").
- Contact us if still missing.

### "I didn't receive my referral points"

- Check if your friend's order is "Completed".
- Check if your friend entered your code at signup.
- Wait 48 hours after your friend's first order.
- Contact us if still missing.

### "My points expired"

- Check expiration policy (24 months inactivity for Standard).
- Make a purchase to reset the timer.
- Once expired, points cannot be restored.
