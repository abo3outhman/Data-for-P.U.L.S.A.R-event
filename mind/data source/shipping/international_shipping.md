# DZShop — International Shipping

## Overview

DZShop currently focuses on the Algerian market. However, we offer **limited international shipping** for selected products to specific countries.

## ⚠️ Important Notice

> **DZShop does NOT ship internationally to individual customers (B2C).**
>
> International shipping is only available for:
> 1. Algerian expatriates sending gifts to family in Algeria (reverse shipping).
> 2. B2B customers ordering in bulk for export.
> 3. Diaspora customers ordering products for delivery to Algeria.

## Service 1: Diaspora Orders (Foreign Customer → Algerian Recipient)

This is our most popular "international" service:

### How It Works
1. An Algerian living abroad orders on `dzshop.dz`.
2. They provide an Algerian delivery address (family, friend).
3. They pay with an international payment method (see below).
4. The order is delivered to the recipient in Algeria.

### Payment Methods for Diaspora Customers

| Method | Available? | Notes |
|--------|------------|-------|
| International Visa/Mastercard | ❌ Not directly | Use a third-party service |
| PayPal | ❌ Not supported in Algeria | |
| Bank Transfer (International) | ✅ Yes | SWIFT transfer to DZShop's account |
| Western Union | ❌ Not for online orders | |
| Ria Money Transfer | ❌ Not for online orders | |
| MoneyGram | ❌ Not for online orders | |
| Algerian Embassy Payment | ✅ Yes | For diaspora in countries with Algerian embassies |

### Bank Transfer Process for Diaspora

1. Place the order on dzshop.dz with "Bank Transfer" as payment.
2. You'll receive an email with DZShop's bank details (SWIFT/BIC, IBAN).
3. Make an international SWIFT transfer from your bank.
4. Send the transfer proof to `payments@dzshop.dz`.
5. We process the order once the funds arrive (3–7 business days).

### Diaspora-Specific Features

- ✅ Order in Arabic, French, or English.
- ✅ Currency converter on the website (DZD ↔ EUR, USD, GBP, CAD).
- ✅ Send a personalized message with the order (printed card included).
- ✅ Gift wrapping option (+300 DZD).
- ✅ Schedule delivery for a specific date (birthday, Eid, etc.).

## Service 3: B2B International Export

For businesses outside Algeria wanting to buy Algerian products:

### Eligible Products for Export

- ✅ Algerian-made products (dates, olive oil, traditional crafts).
- ✅ Products from Algerian brands with export authorization.
- ❌ Electronics (most are imported, not eligible for re-export).
- ❌ Counterfeit or restricted products.

### Minimum Order for B2B Export

- **Minimum value**: 100,000 DZD (~680 EUR).
- **Minimum quantity**: Per product requirements (varies).

### B2B Export Process

1. Contact `b2b@dzshop.dz` with your business details.
2. Provide:
   - Company name and registration number.
   - Country of destination.
   - Product list and quantities.
   - Import license (if required by your country).
3. We send a quote including:
   - Product prices.
   - Export documentation fees.
   - International shipping fees.
   - Customs duties (estimated).
4. You confirm the order and pay by international bank transfer.
5. We prepare the order (5–15 business days).
6. We handle export documentation (Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary, etc.).
7. We ship via:
   - **Air freight** (DHL, FedEx, Aramex): 5–10 days.
   - **Sea freight**: 20–45 days.
8. You receive the order and clear customs in your country.

### B2B Export Documentation

We provide:

- ✅ Commercial Invoice.
- ✅ Packing List.
- ✅ Certificate of Origin (issued by Algerian Chamber of Commerce).
- ✅ Phytosanitary Certificate (for food products).
- ✅ Certificate of Free Sale (for cosmetics/health products).
- ❌ Certificate of Halal (only for products certified by Algerian Islamic Authority).

### B2B Shipping Costs

- Customs broker fees.

## Customs and Import Duties

### For Diaspora Orders (gifts to Algeria)
- No customs duties for gifts under 5,000 DZD.
- Standard customs may apply for orders above 5,000 DZD.
- DZShop handles customs clearance for the recipient.

### For B2B Export
- The **importer** (B2B customer) is responsible for customs clearance in their country.
- We provide all necessary documentation.
- We can recommend customs brokers in major countries.

## Countries We Do NOT Ship To

For B2B export, we cannot ship to:

- ❌ Countries under UN sanctions.
