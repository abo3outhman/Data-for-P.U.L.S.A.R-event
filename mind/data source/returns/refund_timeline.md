# DZShop — Refund Timeline

## Overview

This document provides detailed timelines for refunds based on the original payment method and refund type.

## Standard Refund Timelines

| Original Payment Method | Refund Method | Processing Time |
|------------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| CIB card | Credit to same card | 5–7 business days |
| Edahabia | Credit to same card | 5–7 business days |
| BaridiMob | Credit to same account | 5–7 business days |
| DZShop Wallet | Credit to wallet | 24 hours |
| Cash on Delivery (COD) | Bank transfer or DZShop Wallet | 7–10 business days |
| Bank Transfer | Bank transfer | 7–10 business days |
| Installments | Refund to original schedule | 10–15 business days |
| Gift Card | Credit to gift card balance | Instant |

## Refund Timeline by Payment Method (Detailed)

### CIB Card Refunds
1. DZShop initiates refund via SATIM.
2. SATIM processes the refund request (1–2 business days).
3. Customer's bank receives the refund (3–5 business days).
4. Customer's bank credits the card (1–2 business days).
5. **Total: 5–7 business days** (sometimes up to 10).

> ⚠️ Weekends and public holidays are NOT business days.

### Edahabia Card Refunds
1. DZShop initiates refund via Algérie Poste.
2. Algérie Poste processes (2–3 business days).
3. Card credited (2–4 business days).
4. **Total: 5–7 business days**.

### BaridiMob Refunds
1. DZShop initiates refund.
2. BaridiMob processes (1–2 business days).
3. Account credited (3–5 business days).
4. **Total: 5–7 business days**.

### DZShop Wallet Refunds
1. DZShop credits the wallet internally.
2. Funds available immediately.
3. Customer can use the funds for new orders instantly.
4. Customer can request withdrawal to bank account (5–7 business days).
5. **Total: Instant (24 hours max for processing)**.

### Cash on Delivery (COD) Refunds
- Cannot refund in cash (we don't have the cash; the delivery partner does).
- Refund options:
  1. **Bank transfer**: Customer provides RIB. Refund in 7–10 business days.
  2. **DZShop Wallet**: Refund in 24 hours (recommended).
  3. **Store pickup**: Customer picks up cash at any DZShop store (within 48 hours of approval, requires ID).

### Bank Transfer Refunds
1. DZShop initiates transfer.
2. Bank processes (1–3 business days).
3. Funds arrive in customer's account (3–7 business days).
4. **Total: 7–10 business days**.

### Installment Plan Refunds

#### Before First Payment
- Order cancelled, no refund needed.

#### After First Payment (Single Installment)
1. DZShop refunds the paid installment.
2. Future installments are cancelled.
3. Refund processed in 10–15 business days.

#### After Multiple Installments
1. DZShop calculates total paid.
2. All paid installments refunded.
3. Future installments cancelled.
4. Refund processed in 10–15 business days.

#### After All Installments Paid
1. Full refund of all installments.
2. Processing time: 10–15 business days.

## How to Check Refund Status

### Method 1: Website
1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to **My Orders**.
3. Select the order with the refund.
4. Click **Refund Status**.

### Method 2: Mobile App
1. Open the app.
2. Go to **My Orders**.
3. Select the order.
4. Tap **Refund Details**.

### Method 3: Email
- Refund confirmation email contains a tracking link.
- Click the link to see real-time status.

### Method 4: Hotline
1. Call **3030**.
2. Press **3** for refunds.
3. Provide your order number.

### Method 5: WhatsApp
- Send your order number to +213 770 12 34 56.
- Receive automated status update.

## Common Refund Delays

### Why is my refund taking longer than expected?

1. **Bank processing delays**: Some banks take longer than others.
2. **Weekends/holidays**: Not counted as business days.
3. **Missing information**: For COD refunds, we need your RIB.
4. **Verification needed**: For large refunds or suspected fraud.
5. **Payment processor delays**: SATIM or Algérie Poste delays.
6. **Card expired**: We need a new card or alternative method.

### What to Do If Refund Is Late

1. **Check your refund status** (see above).
2. **Verify the refund method** (card vs. wallet vs. bank).
3. **Check your bank statement** for "DZShop Refund" or "SATIM Refund".
4. **Wait 3 extra business days** (banks sometimes delay).
5. **Contact us** with your refund tracking ID:
   - Email: `payments@dzshop.dz`
   - Hotline: 3030
   - WhatsApp: +213 770 12 34 56

We investigate all late refunds within **48 hours**.
