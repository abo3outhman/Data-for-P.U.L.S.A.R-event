# DZShop — Exchange Policy

## Overview

Customers can exchange products for a different size, color, or model, subject to the conditions below.

## Exchange Window

| Product Type | Exchange Window | Condition |
|--------------|-----------------|-----------|
| Most products | 7 calendar days | From delivery date |
| Fashion items | 7 calendar days | Tags attached, unworn |
| Electronics | 7 calendar days | Original packaging + accessories |
| Defective products | 30 calendar days | For replacement |

> ⚠️ Exchanges are subject to the same conditions as returns (see `return_policy.md`).

## What Can Be Exchanged?

### Allowed Exchanges

| Exchange Type | Conditions |
|---------------|------------|
| **Size exchange** (fashion) | Same product, different size |
| **Color exchange** | Same product, different color (if available) |
| **Model exchange** | Different model (price difference must be paid or refunded) |
| **Replacement for defective** | Same product (or equivalent if out of stock) |

### Not Allowed

- ❌ Exchanging for a completely different product (must return and reorder).
- ❌ Exchanging opened beauty/personal care products.
- ❌ Exchanging customized products.
- ❌ Exchanging digital products.
- ❌ Exchanging "Final Sale" items.

## Exchange Process

### Step 1: Initiate the Exchange

Choose one of these methods:

#### Online (Website/App)
1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to **My Orders**.
3. Select the order with the product to exchange.
4. Click **Exchange Item**.
5. Select the item to exchange.
6. Choose the exchange reason:
   - Wrong size
   - Wrong color
   - Defective
   - Want different model
7. Select the new size/color/model (from available options).
8. Submit the request.

#### Phone (Hotline)
1. Call **3030**.
2. Press **3** for exchanges.
3. Provide your order number.
4. Tell the agent what you want to exchange and what you want instead.

#### WhatsApp
1. Send a message to **+213 770 12 34 56**.
2. Send: "EXCHANGE [order number] [item] [what you want]".
3. A customer service agent will assist you.

#### In-Store
1. Bring the product to any of our 5 stores.
2. Bring the original receipt and ID.
3. The store staff will process the exchange immediately (if the new item is in stock).

### Step 2: Wait for Approval

- We review the exchange request within **24 hours**.
- We check if the requested new item is in stock.
- You'll receive an email with:
  - Approval (or rejection with reason).
  - Exchange Authorization Number (EAN).
  - Instructions for returning the original product.

### Step 3: Return the Original Product

- Follow the same process as returns (see `return_policy.md`).
- Return shipping options:
  - Drop at DZShop store: **Free**.
  - Yalidine pickup: 350 DZD (you pay, we refund if exchange is approved).
  - DZShop Express (Algiers): **Free**.

### Step 4: We Ship the New Product

- Once we receive the original product, we ship the new one.
- Shipping is **free** for exchanges (any shipping option).
- You'll receive a tracking link.

### Step 5: Receive the New Product

- The new product is delivered to your address.
- You sign the delivery receipt.
- Exchange is complete.

## Price Difference Handling

### If the New Product Is More Expensive
- You pay the price difference.
- Payment methods: Same as original order (CIB, Edahabia, Wallet, etc.).
- The difference is charged before the new product is shipped.

### If the New Product Is Cheaper
- We refund the price difference.
- Refund method: Same as original payment.
- Refund timeline: See `refund_timeline.md`.

### Example
- Original product: Shirt (size M) — 3,500 DZD.
- New product: Same shirt (size L) — 3,500 DZD.
- **Price difference: 0 DZD** — simple exchange.

### Example 2
- Original product: Phone (Model A) — 45,000 DZD.
- New product: Phone (Model B) — 55,000 DZD.
- **Price difference: +10,000 DZD** — you pay 10,000 DZD.

### Example 3
- Original product: TV (55") — 120,000 DZD.
- New product: TV (50") — 95,000 DZD.
- **Price difference: -25,000 DZD** — we refund 25,000 DZD.

## Special Cases

### Defective Product Exchange
- If the product is defective (DZShop's fault):
  - Return shipping is **free**.
  - Exchange shipping is **free**.
  - You can request a **replacement** (same product).
  - Or request a **refund** instead.
  - Compensation: 500 DZD store credit.

### Wrong Product Received
- If we shipped the wrong product:
  - Free return and free exchange.
  - We ship the correct product immediately (don't wait for return).
  - Compensation: 500 DZD store credit.

### Exchange Out of Stock
- If the product you want is out of stock:
  1. We notify you within 24 hours.
  2. Options:
     - Wait for restock (we'll ship when available).
     - Choose a different size/color/model.
     - Get a full refund instead.
  3. You choose, and we proceed.

## Exchange for Installment Orders

If the original order was paid with installments:

### Same Price Exchange
- No payment changes.
- Installments continue as before.

### Higher Price Exchange
- Pay the difference upfront.
- Installments continue for the original amount.

### Lower Price Exchange
- Difference is refunded to the installment schedule.
- Future installments are reduced proportionally.
- Or the difference is refunded in full (10–15 business days).

## Common Issues

### "I want to exchange for a product that's no longer available"
- We'll offer alternatives or a full refund.

### "I missed the 7-day exchange window"
- After 7 days, exchanges are not possible.
- You can still request a return (within 7 days) for a refund.

### "I exchanged but I don't like the new product either"
- You can exchange again, but it counts as a second exchange.
- After 2 exchanges on the same order, only refunds are allowed.

### "The exchanged product is defective"
- Treated as a defective product (free return + free exchange + compensation).
