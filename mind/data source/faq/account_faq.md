# DZShop — Account FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions (Account)

### Q1: How do I create a DZShop account?
**A:** 
1. Go to `dzshop.dz` and click **"Sign Up"**.
2. Fill in the form: first name, last name, email, phone, password.
3. Accept Terms and Conditions.
4. Click **"Create Account"**.
5. Verify your email (click the link in the verification email).
6. Verify your phone (enter the SMS code).

See `account/create_account.md` for full details.

### Q2: Is creating an account free?
**A:** Yes, creating a DZShop account is completely **free**. There are no monthly fees or hidden charges.

### Q3: Can I order without an account?
**A:** Yes, you can check out as a guest. However, we recommend creating an account for:
- Faster checkout.
- Order tracking.
- Loyalty points (DZShop Points).
- Wishlist.
- Order history.

### Q4: I forgot my password. What do I do?
**A:** 
1. Go to `dzshop.dz/reset-password`.
2. Enter your email or phone number.
3. Receive a reset link by email (or code by SMS).
4. Click the link and set a new password.

See `account/password_reset.md` for full details.

### Q5: My account is locked. What do I do?
**A:** After 5 failed login attempts, your account is locked for **30 minutes**. To unlock:
- Wait 30 minutes, OR
- Use "Forgot Password" to reset your password (this also unlocks the account), OR
- Contact support at 3030 if urgent.

### Q6: Can I have multiple DZShop accounts?
**A:** Technically yes, but:
- Each account must have a unique email and phone number.
- Loyalty points are NOT transferable between accounts.
- We recommend one account per person.
- Multiple accounts for fraudulent purposes will be suspended.

### Q7: How do I change my account information?
**A:** 
1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to **My Account** → **Profile**.
3. Update your information (name, email, phone, address).
4. Save changes.

For email changes, you'll need to verify the new email. For phone changes, you'll need to verify via SMS.

### Q8: How do I delete my account?
**A:** 
1. Go to **My Account** → **Settings** → **Delete Account**.
2. Confirm your password.
3. Select a reason for deletion.
4. Click **"Delete My Account"**.

> ⚠️ Account deletion is permanent. All data, order history, and loyalty points will be lost. Pending orders and refunds will still be processed.

By Algerian law (Law 18-07), we retain some transactional data for 5 years for legal/tax purposes.

### Q9: Can I log in with Google or Facebook?
**A:** Yes, DZShop supports social login with Google and Facebook. To use:
1. On the login page, click **"Continue with Google"** or **"Continue with Facebook"**.
2. Authorize DZShop to access your basic profile info.
3. You'll be logged in.

> ⚠️ Social login is only available if you previously linked your social account to DZShop.

### Q10: How do I enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?
**A:** 
1. Go to **My Account** → **Security**.
2. Click **"Enable 2FA"**.
3. Choose method: SMS or Authenticator App.
4. Follow the setup instructions.
5. 2FA is required for:
   - Login from a new device.
   - Changing account information.
   - Orders above 50,000 DZD.

## Account Security Tips

✅ **DO:**
- Use a strong, unique password.
- Enable 2FA.
- Log out from shared/public computers.
- Keep your email and phone updated.
- Monitor your order history for suspicious activity.

❌ **DON'T:**
- Don't share your password with anyone (including DZShop staff).
- Don't use public Wi-Fi for payments without VPN.
- Don't click suspicious links in emails.
- Don't save passwords in browsers on shared computers.
