# DZShop — General FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions (General)

### Q1: What is DZShop?
**A:** DZShop is a leading Algerian e-commerce platform founded in 2020, headquartered in Algiers. We sell products in 8 categories (Electronics, Fashion, Home & Kitchen, Beauty & Health, Sports & Outdoor, Toys & Baby, Books & Stationery, and Groceries pilot in Algiers) to customers in all 58 wilayas of Algeria.

### Q2: Is DZShop a legitimate company?
**A:** Yes. DZShop is registered as DZShop SPA with:
- Commercial Register (RC): 16/00-1234567 B
- Tax ID (NIF): 000116123456789
- Capital: 10,000,000 DZD
- Headquarters: Hydra, Algiers
We are certified by SATIM, ARPT, and are a member of CACI.

### Q3: Do I need to create an account to order?
**A:** No, you can check out as a guest. However, we recommend creating an account for:
- Faster checkout
- Order tracking
- Loyalty points (DZShop Points)
- Wishlist
- Order history

### Q4: Can I order from outside Algeria?
**A:** Yes, diaspora customers can order products for delivery to recipients in Algeria. Payment is by international bank transfer. See `shipping/international_shipping.md` for details.

### Q5: What are your working hours?
**A:** 
- **Customer Service (Hotline 3030, WhatsApp)**: Saturday to Thursday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM. Friday: closed.
- **WhatsApp extended**: Saturday to Thursday, 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM. Friday: 2:00 PM – 10:00 PM (limited).
- **Website/App**: Available 24/7 (automated).
- **Physical stores**: Saturday to Thursday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Friday: closed.

### Q6: In which languages can I contact customer service?
**A:** Our customer service team speaks:
- Arabic (Algerian dialect and Modern Standard Arabic)
- French
- Tamazight (for stores in Kabylie regions)

### Q7: How can I find a product on DZShop?
**A:** You can:
1. Use the **search bar** at the top of the website.
2. **Browse by category** from the main menu.
3. Use **filters** (price, brand, rating, availability).
4. Use the **mobile app** search feature (with voice search).

### Q8: Can I see the products in person before buying?
**A:** Yes, you can visit any of our 5 physical stores (Algiers, Oran, Constantine, Sétif, Annaba) to see selected products. Use the **Click & Collect** option to order online and pick up in-store.

### Q9: What if a product is out of stock?
**A:** If a product is out of stock:
- You'll see "Out of Stock" on the product page.
- You can click **"Notify Me"** to receive an email when it's back in stock.
- Some products allow **pre-order** with a 20% deposit.

### Q10: Are the prices on the website the same as in-store?
**A:** Not always. In-store prices are usually **slightly lower** (no shipping fees, no online processing fees). Online prices include VAT but exclude shipping.

### Q11: Do you offer gift wrapping?
**A:** Yes, gift wrapping is available for **300 DZD** per item. You can add it at checkout. You can also include a personalized message (free).

### Q12: Can I schedule a delivery for a specific date?
**A:** Yes, you can:
- Choose a specific delivery date at checkout (for Standard, Economy, and Budget options).
- The date must be at least 3 days after the order date.
- Available for all wilayas.
- Not available for DZShop Express (same-day).

### Q13: Do you have a mobile app?
**A:** Yes, the DZShop app is available on:
- **Android**: Google Play Store
- **iOS**: Apple App Store
The app offers exclusive features:
- Push notifications for orders.
- Voice search.
- Faster checkout with biometric login.
- App-only promotions.

### Q14: How do I unsubscribe from the newsletter?
**A:** To unsubscribe:
1. Open any newsletter email.
2. Click **"Unsubscribe"** at the bottom.
3. Confirm your unsubscription.
OR
1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to **Settings** → **Notifications**.
3. Uncheck "Email newsletter".

### Q15: Can I buy a gift card?
**A:** Yes, we offer:
- **Digital gift cards**: 1,000 – 50,000 DZD, sent by email within 24 hours.
- **Physical gift cards**: 1,000 – 50,000 DZD, mailed to the recipient (5–10 days).
Gift cards can be purchased at `dzshop.dz/gift-cards`.

### Q16: Do you offer student discounts?
**A:** Yes, students with a valid student ID get **10% off** year-round. To apply:
1. Create an account.
2. Go to **My Account** → **Verification** → **Student**.
3. Upload your student ID.
4. Verification takes 24–48 hours.
5. Once verified, the discount is automatic at checkout.

### Q17: Do you have a B2B program for businesses?
**A:** Yes, our B2B program offers:
- Special pricing.
- Dedicated account manager.
- Invoice billing.
- Bulk shipping rates.
Apply at `dzshop.dz/b2b` or email `b2b@dzshop.dz`.

### Q18: How can I provide feedback or complain?
**A:** You can submit feedback or complaints through:
1. **Online form**: `dzshop.dz/feedback`
2. **Email**: feedback@dzshop.dz
3. **Phone**: hotline 3030 (option 4)
4. **In-store**: at any of our 5 stores

All complaints are reviewed within **48 hours**, and a response is provided within **5 business days**.

### Q19: Can I become a DZShop seller?
**A:** Yes, we have a seller program for Algerian businesses. To apply:
1. Visit `dzshop.dz/sell`.
2. Fill in the application.
3. Provide your business documents (RC, NIF, etc.).
4. Our team reviews within 5 business days.
5. If approved, you can list your products on DZShop.
