# DZShop — Coupon Codes

## Overview

This document lists all active coupon codes at DZShop, their conditions, and how to use them.

> ⚠️ Coupons are subject to change. We update this document every Monday.

## How to Use a Coupon Code

### Step-by-Step

1. Add products to your cart.
2. Click the cart icon → **"Proceed to Checkout"**.
3. On the checkout page, find the **"Coupon Code"** field.
4. Enter the code exactly as written (case-sensitive).
5. Click **"Apply"**.
6. The discount is applied to your order total.
7. Continue with checkout.

### Important Notes

- Only **one coupon code** per order.
- Coupons are **case-sensitive** (`AUTUMN25` works, `autumn25` doesn't).
- Coupons have expiration dates.
- Some coupons require a **minimum order amount**.
- Some coupons are **category-specific**.
- Some coupons are **customer-specific** (sent by email).

## Coupon Code Format

DZShop coupon codes follow this pattern:

```
[NAME][NUMBER]
```

Where:
- `NAME` is a descriptive word (AUTUMN, WELCOME, VIP).
- `NUMBER` is a percentage or year (25, 10, 2025).

**Examples:**
- `AUTUMN25` — Autumn sale, 25% (or up to 40%).
- `WELCOME10` — New customer, 10%.
- `VIPTECH20` — VIP, electronics, 20%.
- `NEWYEAR2026` — New Year 2026.

## Coupon Code Rules

### What You CAN Do
- ✅ Use one coupon per order.
- ✅ Combine with automatic offers (e.g., free shipping).
- ✅ Use on sale items (unless specified otherwise).
- ✅ Use on installment orders.
- ✅ Use for Click & Collect orders.

### What You CANNOT Do
- ❌ Use multiple coupons on one order.
- ❌ Use an expired coupon.
- ❌ Use a coupon below the minimum order.
- ❌ Use a coupon on a non-eligible category.
- ❌ Use a VIP coupon if you're not VIP.
- ❌ Use a coupon on an already-placed order.
- ❌ Sell or transfer coupons to other customers.
- ❌ Use a coupon on "Final Sale" items (unless specified).

## Coupon Not Working?

### Common Reasons

1. **Expired**: The coupon's validity period has ended.
2. **Below minimum**: Your order total is below the minimum.
3. **Wrong category**: The coupon doesn't apply to your products.
4. **Typo**: You misspelled the code (case-sensitive).
5. **Not logged in**: Some coupons require an account.
6. **Already used**: One-time coupons can't be reused.
7. **Not eligible**: You don't meet the customer criteria (e.g., VIP).
8. **Combined with another coupon**: Only one coupon per order.

### What to Do

1. Double-check the code spelling.
2. Check the expiration date.
3. Check the minimum order.
4. Check the category restrictions.
5. Remove other coupons from the order.
6. Log in to your account.
7. If still not working, contact us:
   - Email: `support@dzshop.dz`
   - Hotline: 3030
   - WhatsApp: +213 770 12 34 56

## Refunds for Coupon Orders

If you return a product that was bought with a coupon:

- The coupon discount is **proportional** to the returned items.
- The refund amount = (item price) - (proportional coupon discount).
- The coupon is **not re-credited** to your account (unless it's a customer-specific coupon and the order is fully cancelled).

### Example
- Order: 2 shirts × 5,000 DZD = 10,000 DZD.
- Coupon: `DRESSES25` (25% off) → discount: 2,500 DZD.
- Paid: 7,500 DZD.
- Return 1 shirt:
  - Item price: 5,000 DZD.
  - Proportional discount: 1,250 DZD.
  - Refund: 5,000 - 1,250 = **3,750 DZD**.
