## Stop Desk Locations

Stop Desks are partner locations where customers can pick up their orders. Available in 45 wilayas:

### Major Stop Desk Networks

| Partner | Coverage | Number of Desks |
|---------|----------|-----------------|
| Yalidine Stop Desk | 45 wilayas | 850+ desks |
| Maystro Stop Desk | 30 wilayas | 420+ desks |
| Algérie Poste | All 58 wilayas | 3,800+ post offices |
| DZShop Stores | 5 wilayas | 5 stores |

### Finding a Stop Desk

Customers can find the nearest Stop Desk by:

1. **Website**: `dzshop.dz/stop-desks` (enter wilaya code).
2. **Mobile App**: Use GPS to find nearest desk.
3. **Hotline**: 3030, option 6.
4. **WhatsApp**: +213 770 12 34 56 (with wilaya code).

## Military Zones

The following areas are NOT served by DZShop:

- Active military bases.
- Restricted security zones.
- Areas under curfew (if any).

If your address is in such an area, you can:
1. Provide an alternative address in a nearby commune.
2. Pick up from the nearest Stop Desk.

## Commune Coverage

Each wilaya has multiple communes (dairas and baladiyas). To check if your commune is served:

1. Go to `dzshop.dz/coverage`.
2. Select your wilaya.
3. View the list of served communes.
4. If your commune is not listed, contact us for alternatives.

## Unserved Areas

If your area is not served at all:

1. **Email**: `support@dzshop.dz` with your address.
2. We'll suggest the nearest pickup point or alternative.
3. In rare cases, we may add your area to our coverage (depending on demand).
