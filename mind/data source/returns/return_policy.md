# DZShop — Return Policy

## Overview

This document outlines the complete return policy for DZShop. For the product-level overview, see `products/returns_policy.md`.

## Return Window

| Product Type | Return Window | Condition |
|--------------|---------------|-----------|
| Most products | 7 calendar days | From delivery date |
| Electronics | 7 calendar days | Original packaging + accessories |
| Fashion items | 7 calendar days | Tags attached, unworn |
| Defective products | 30 calendar days | DZShop's responsibility |
| Wrong products received | 30 calendar days | DZShop's mistake |
| Customized products | ❌ No returns | Personalized items |
| Digital products | ❌ No returns | eBooks, gift cards, software |

## Return Eligibility Conditions

### Conditions for a Valid Return

1. The product must be returned within **7 calendar days** of delivery.
2. The product must be in its **original condition**:
   - Unused (or only tested for proper functionality).
   - In original packaging (box, manuals, accessories).
   - Tags attached (for fashion items).
   - No scratches, dents, or damage caused by the customer.
3. The original receipt or order number must be included.
4. The product must not be on the **non-returnable list**.

### Non-Returnable Products

For hygiene, safety, or legal reasons, the following cannot be returned:

#### Hygiene Reasons
- Opened personal care products (deodorants, razors, toothbrushes).
- Opened skincare and beauty products.
- Underwear and swimwear.
- Pillows and mattresses (unless unopened).
- Opened food and grocery items.

#### Customized Products
- Engraved items.
- Custom-printed products.
- Made-to-measure clothing.
- Personalized gifts.

#### Digital Products
- eBook downloads.
- Software licenses.
- Gift cards (digital and physical).
- Subscription services.

#### Final Sale Items
- Products marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance".
- Products sold with a discount of 50% or more.

## Return Process

### Step 1: Initiate the Return

Choose one of these methods:

#### Online (Website/App)
1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to **My Orders**.
3. Select the order containing the product to return.
4. Click **Return Item**.
5. Select the item(s) to return.
6. Choose a return reason from the dropdown.
7. Add photos (required for damaged/defective products).
8. Submit the request.

#### Phone (Hotline)
1. Call **3030**.
2. Press **3** for returns.
3. Provide your order number.
4. Tell the agent which item(s) you want to return and why.
5. The agent will create the return request.

#### WhatsApp
1. Send a message to **+213 770 12 34 56**.
2. Send: "RETURN [order number] [item name] [reason]".
3. A customer service agent will assist you (during working hours).

#### Email
1. Email `returns@dzshop.dz`.
2. Subject: "Return Request — [Order Number]".
3. Include:
   - Order number.
   - Item(s) to return.
   - Reason for return.
   - Photos (for damaged/defective items).
4. Response within 24 hours.

#### In-Store
1. Bring the product to any of our 5 stores.
2. Bring the original receipt (or order number) and ID.
3. The store staff will process the return immediately.

### Step 2: Wait for Approval

- Our team reviews the return request within **24 hours**.
- You'll receive an email with:
  - Approval (or rejection with reason).
  - Return Authorization Number (RAN).
  - Return instructions.

### Step 3: Package the Product

- Use the original packaging if possible.
- Include all accessories, manuals, and free gifts.
- Write the **Return Authorization Number** on the package.
- Do not tape or write on the original product box (use a separate outer box).
- Take photos of the packaged product (for your records).

### Step 4: Ship the Product

Choose a return shipping method:

| Method | Cost | Time |
|--------|------|------|
| Drop at any DZShop store | **Free** | Same day |
| Yalidine pickup (we arrange) | 350 DZD (deducted from refund) | 24–48 hours |
| Algérie Télécom (we arrange) | 200 DZD (deducted from refund) | 5–7 days |
| DZShop Express (Algiers) | **Free** | Same day |
| Your own shipping method | Your cost | Variable |

> 💡 We recommend dropping the product at one of our stores for fastest processing.

### Step 5: We Receive and Inspect

- We receive the product at our Hydra warehouse.
- Our team inspects the product within **48 hours**.
- Inspection includes:
  - Verify the product matches the order.
  - Check the condition (used, damaged, missing parts).
  - Test functionality (for electronics).

### Step 6: Refund Processing

- If approved, the refund is initiated within **24 hours** of inspection.
- The refund is sent to the original payment method.
- Processing time depends on the payment method (see `refund_timeline.md`).

## Return Reasons

When initiating a return, choose one of these reasons:

1. **Changed my mind** — Product is fine, but I don't want it.
2. **Wrong size** — Doesn't fit (fashion items).
3. **Wrong color** — Different from what I expected.
4. **Product defective** — Doesn't work properly.
5. **Wrong product received** — I received a different product.
6. **Product damaged** — Arrived damaged.
7. **Not as described** — Different from the website description.
8. **Found a better price** — Cheaper elsewhere.
9. **Ordered by mistake** — Didn't mean to order.
10. **Other** — Specify in the comments.

## Return Outcomes

### Approved Return
- Refund is processed.
- Refund amount based on original payment method.
- Confirmation email sent.

### Partially Approved
- Some items are returned, others are rejected (with reason).
- Refund covers only approved items.
- Rejected items are shipped back to the customer (free of charge).

### Rejected Return
- The product is shipped back to the customer (free of charge).
- Reasons for rejection:
  - Product not in original condition.
  - Return window expired.
  - Product on non-returnable list.
  - Damage caused by the customer.
- The customer can appeal within 7 days by emailing `disputes@dzshop.dz`.

## Special Cases

### Defective Product (DZShop's Fault)
- Return shipping is **free** (we cover all costs).
- Refund is processed within **48 hours** of receiving the return.
- You can request a **replacement** instead of a refund.
- You may receive a **store credit** of 500 DZD as compensation.

### Wrong Product Received
- Same as defective product (free return, fast refund).
- We send the correct product (if available) or full refund.
- Compensation: 500 DZD store credit.

### Damaged in Transit
1. **Refuse the delivery** if damage is visible (best option).
2. If accepted, take photos within 48 hours.
3. Contact us at `returns@dzshop.dz`.
4. We arrange free pickup and replacement (or refund).

### Lost Package
- If your package is lost in transit (confirmed missing after 30 days):
- Full refund issued.
- Compensation: 1,000 DZD store credit.

## Return Fraud Prevention

DZShop reserves the right to refuse returns in cases of suspected fraud:

- Returning a different product than purchased.
- Returning empty boxes.
- Returning counterfeit products.
- Repeated returns of the same product type.
- Returning products with switched parts.

Customers suspected of fraud will be contacted, and their account may be suspended pending investigation.
