# DZShop — Same-Day Delivery (DZShop Express)

## Overview

**DZShop Express** is our premium same-day delivery service, available **only in Algiers** (wilaya 16). Orders placed before 14:00 are delivered within 4 hours.

## Service Details

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Service Name** | DZShop Express |
| **Coverage** | Algiers wilaya (16) — all 57 communes |
| **Delivery Time** | Within 4 hours of order placement |
| **Operating Hours** | Saturday – Thursday: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM |
| **Friday** | Closed |
| **Cost** | 600 DZD (flat rate, up to 5 kg) |
| **Tracking** | Real-time GPS tracking |

## How It Works

### Step 1: Place Your Order
1. Add products to your cart.
2. At checkout, select **"DZShop Express (Same Day)"** as the shipping option.
3. The option appears only if:
   - Your delivery address is in Algiers.
   - All products in your cart are eligible (see below).
   - You placed the order before 14:00.

### Step 2: Order Processing
- Order is prioritized in our warehouse.
- Products are picked and packed within 30 minutes.
- A DZShop Express courier is assigned.

### Step 3: Courier Pickup
- Courier picks up the package from our Hydra warehouse.
- You receive an SMS with the courier's name and phone number.
- Tracking link is activated.

### Step 4: Delivery
- Courier delivers to your address.
- You can track the courier's location in real-time.
- Estimated arrival time is shown.

### Step 5: Confirmation
- You (or someone you authorized) receive the package.
- For COD orders: Pay the courier in cash.
- You sign the delivery receipt.
- Delivery is marked as "Completed" in your account.

## Eligibility

### Geographic Eligibility
- ✅ All 57 communes of Algiers wilaya.
- ❌ No other wilayas are served by DZShop Express.

### Product Eligibility
- ✅ Most products (smartphones, clothing, books, accessories).
- ❌ Large appliances (refrigerators, washing machines).
- ❌ Furniture.
- ❌ Groceries with cold chain requirements (we only sell non-perishable groceries).
- ❌ Pre-order products.

### Weight Limit
- ✅ Up to 5 kg: Standard fee (600 DZD).
- ✅ 5–15 kg: +200 DZD (800 DZD total).
- ✅ 15–30 kg: +600 DZD (1,200 DZD total).
- ❌ 30+ kg: Not available.

### Time Limit
- Orders placed **before 14:00**: Delivered same day.
- Orders placed **after 14:00**: Delivered next day (before 12:00).
- Orders placed **on Friday**: Delivered Saturday (before 12:00).

## Delivery Time Guarantee

We guarantee delivery within **4 hours** or your shipping fee is refunded.

### Guarantee Conditions
- Order placed before 14:00.
- All products in stock.
- Delivery address in Algiers.
- Weather conditions allow safe delivery.
- Customer is reachable by phone.

### How to Claim the Guarantee
If your order is delivered after 4 hours:

1. Email `support@dzshop.dz` within 24 hours.
2. Include your order number.
3. Mention "Late Delivery Guarantee".
4. Our team will verify and refund the 600 DZD shipping fee within 5 business days.

> ⚠️ The guarantee covers the shipping fee only, not the order amount.

## Tracking Your Same-Day Delivery

DZShop Express offers **real-time GPS tracking**:

1. **Live Map**: See the courier's current location on a map.
2. **ETA**: Estimated time of arrival (updated in real-time).
3. **Courier Info**: Name, photo, phone number.
4. **Status Updates**: Picked up, on the way, arriving.

### How to Access Tracking

- **Email**: Click the tracking link in the shipment email.
- **SMS**: Click the tracking link in the SMS.
- **App**: Open DZShop app → My Orders → Track.
- **Website**: Log in → My Orders → Track.

## Delivery Instructions

When placing your order, you can add delivery instructions:

- "Ring the bell twice."
- "Leave with the concierge."
- "Call before arriving."
- "Deliver to the back door."

These instructions are sent to the courier.

## Courier Communication

You can contact the courier directly:

- **Phone**: The courier's phone number is shared via SMS.
- **WhatsApp**: Some couriers accept WhatsApp messages.
- **In-app chat**: Limited (during delivery only).

> ⚠️ Please do not call the courier before they pick up your package. They will call you when they're on the way.

## Failed Same-Day Delivery

If the courier cannot deliver (you're not home, wrong address, etc.):

### First Attempt
- Courier waits 10 minutes.
- Calls you on the phone.
- If no response, leaves a notice.

### Second Attempt (Same Day)
- Courier tries again within 2 hours.
- If still failed, the order is held at our Hydra warehouse.

### Pickup at Warehouse
- You can pick up the order from our Hydra warehouse until 20:00 the same day.
- After 20:00, the order is delivered the next day (before 12:00).

### Refund
If you no longer want the order after a failed delivery:
- You can cancel within 24 hours.
- Refund is processed (5–10 business days).

## Rescheduling

If you need to reschedule your delivery:

1. **Before courier pickup**: Modify the order (within 2 hours of placement).
2. **After courier pickup**: Call the courier directly.
3. **For major changes**: Contact support at 3030.

## Special Cases

### Deliveries to Apartment Buildings
- Courier will deliver to your apartment door (with elevator).
- If no elevator, courier delivers to the ground floor (for orders above 15 kg).

### Deliveries to Office Buildings
- Courier will deliver to the reception or your office.
- Security clearance may be required (mention in delivery instructions).

### Deliveries to Universities/Schools
- Courier delivers to the main gate or reception.
- You must be present to receive the package.

### Deliveries to Hospitals
- Courier delivers to the main reception.
- You must authorize the reception to receive on your behalf.

## Weather and Force Majeure

In case of severe weather (heavy rain, snow, sandstorm), DZShop Express may be suspended:

- Customers are notified by SMS and email.
- Orders are automatically rescheduled to the next day.
- The 4-hour guarantee is suspended during force majeure.
- No fees are charged for the delay.
