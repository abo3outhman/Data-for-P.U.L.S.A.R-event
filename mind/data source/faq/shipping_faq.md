# DZShop — Shipping FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions (Shipping)

### Q1: Which shipping options are available?
**A:** We offer 6 shipping options:
1. **DZShop Express** (same-day, Algiers only) — 4 hours, 600 DZD.
2. **Express (24h)** — Yalidine, 24–72 hours.
3. **Standard (48–72h)** — Yalidine, 2–5 days.
4. **Economy (4–6 days)** — Maystro, 4–7 days.
5. **Budget (5–10 days)** — Algérie Télécom, 5–21 days.
6. **Click & Collect** — Free pickup at our 5 stores.

### Q2: Do you deliver to all 58 wilayas?
**A:** Yes, we deliver to all 58 wilayas. However:
- 41 wilayas have full coverage (all shipping options).
- 14 wilayas have partial coverage (some communes only).
- 3 wilayas have limited coverage (only Algérie Télécom).

See `shipping/delivery_zones.md` for the full list.

### Q3: How long does delivery take in my wilaya?
**A:** See `orders/delivery_time.md` for the full table. Quick summary:
- **Algiers**: 2–4 hours (Express), 2 days (Standard).
- **Major cities** (Oran, Constantine, etc.): 24–72 hours (Express, Standard).
- **Northern wilayas**: 3–5 days (Standard).
- **Saharan wilayas**: 5–21 days (Standard or Budget).

### Q4: How much does shipping cost?
**A:** Shipping costs depend on:
- Weight of the package.
- Destination wilaya.
- Shipping option chosen.
- Stop Desk vs. Home Delivery.

See `shipping/delivery_fees.md` for the full fee table. Quick examples:
- Smartphone to Algiers (Standard, Stop Desk): 350 DZD.
- TV to Oran (Express, Home Delivery): ~1,600 DZD.
- Clothing to Tamanrasset (Budget): ~1,300 DZD.

### Q5: Is there free shipping?
**A:** Yes, in these cases:
- Orders > 15,000 DZD: Free Standard shipping in Algiers.
- Orders > 25,000 DZD: Free Standard shipping in major cities.
- Orders > 50,000 DZD: Free Standard shipping in all covered wilayas.
- VIP customers: Free Express shipping in Algiers.
- Click & Collect: Always free.

### Q6: What is the difference between Stop Desk and Home Delivery?
**A:** 
- **Stop Desk**: You pick up the package from a partner location (Yalidine desk, post office, etc.). Cheaper. You have 7 days to pick up.
- **Home Delivery**: The courier delivers to your address. More expensive. You must be present.

### Q7: How can I track my order?
**A:** You can track your order through:
1. **Website**: `dzshop.dz/track` (enter order number).
2. **Mobile App**: My Orders → Select Order → Track.
3. **Email/SMS**: Click the tracking link.
4. **Hotline**: 3030 (option 1).
5. **WhatsApp**: Send order number to +213 770 12 34 56.
6. **Shipping partner's website** (with tracking number).

See `orders/track_order.md` for full details.

### Q8: What if I'm not home when the courier arrives?
**A:** 
- **First attempt**: Courier leaves a notice and tries again next day.
- **Second attempt**: Another notice.
- **Third attempt**: Package goes to nearest Stop Desk (7 days to pick up).
- **After 7 days**: Package is returned to DZShop. Refund is processed (5–10 business days).

### Q9: Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
**A:** Yes, within **2 hours** of placing the order (and only within the same wilaya). See `orders/modify_order.md` for details.

### Q10: Can I schedule a specific delivery date?
**A:** Yes, for Standard, Economy, and Budget options:
- Choose a date at checkout.
- Date must be at least 3 days after the order date.
- Not available for DZShop Express (same-day).

### Q11: What if my package arrives damaged?
**A:** 
1. **Refuse the delivery** if damage is visible (best option).
2. If you accepted delivery, take photos within **48 hours**.
3. Email `returns@dzshop.dz` with photos and order number.
4. We arrange free pickup and replacement (or refund).

### Q12: Do you ship internationally?
**A:** We don't ship B2C internationally. However:
- Diaspora customers can order for delivery in Algeria (paid by international bank transfer).
- B2B export is available for Algerian-made products.
See `shipping/international_shipping.md` for details.

### Q13: Can I deliver to multiple addresses in one order?
**A:** No. Each order has one delivery address. If you want to send products to multiple addresses, place separate orders.

### Q14: What is Click & Collect?
**A:** Click & Collect allows you to:
1. Order online.
2. Pick up at any of our 5 stores (Algiers, Oran, Constantine, Sétif, Annaba).
3. **Free** (no shipping fee).
4. Ready in **4 hours** (if ordered before 14:00) or next day (if ordered after 14:00).
5. You have **7 days** to pick up.

### Q15: What happens if my delivery is very late?
**A:** 
1. Track your order first.
2. Wait 24 hours past the estimated date.
3. Contact us if still not delivered:
   - Hotline: 3030
   - WhatsApp: +213 770 12 34 56
   - Email: support@dzshop.dz
4. If the delay is significant (more than 7 days), we may offer a **500 DZD store credit** as compensation.

For the **DZShop Express 4-hour guarantee**: if we deliver after 4 hours, your shipping fee is refunded.
