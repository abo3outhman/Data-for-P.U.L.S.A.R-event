# DZShop — Returns Policy

## Overview

At DZShop, we want our customers to be satisfied with their purchases. If you're not happy with a product, you can return it within **7 days** of delivery, subject to the conditions below.

## Return Eligibility

### ✅ Products That CAN Be Returned

- Electronics (in original packaging, with all accessories)
- Fashion items (with tags attached, unwashed, unworn)
- Home & Kitchen products (in original packaging)
- Beauty & Health products (unopened, sealed only)
- Sports & Outdoor equipment (unused)
- Toys & Baby products (in original packaging)
- Books & Stationery (unread, undamaged)

### ❌ Products That CANNOT Be Returned

For hygiene and safety reasons, the following products **cannot be returned**:

- Opened beauty and personal care products
- Opened food and grocery items
- Underwear and swimwear (hygiene reasons)
- Customized or personalized products (e.g., engraved items)
- Products damaged by the customer
- Digital products (eBooks, software licenses)
- Gift cards
- Products sold as "Final Sale" or "Clearance"

## Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return, the product must:

1. Be returned within **7 calendar days** of delivery.
2. Be in its **original condition** (unused, unwashed, with tags).
3. Be in its **original packaging** (box, manuals, accessories).
4. Include the **original receipt** or order number.
5. Not be on the **non-returnable list** above.

## How to Initiate a Return

### Step 1: Submit a Return Request

Customers can submit a return request through:

- **Website**: Log in → My Orders → Select order → "Return Item"
- **Mobile App**: My Orders → Select order → "Return"
- **WhatsApp**: +213 770 12 34 56 (with order number)
- **Phone**: Hotline 3030
- **Email**: returns@dzshop.dz
- **In-store**: At any of our 5 physical stores

### Step 2: Wait for Approval

Our team reviews return requests within **24 hours**. You will receive:
- An approval (or rejection) email
- A return authorization number (if approved)
- Instructions for returning the product

### Step 3: Package the Product

- Pack the product in its original packaging.
- Include all accessories, manuals, and free gifts that came with it.
- Write the return authorization number on the package.
- Do not tape or write on the original product box.

### Step 4: Ship the Product

Customers can return products via:

| Method | Cost | Time |
|--------|------|------|
| **Drop at any DZShop store** | Free | Same day |
| **Yalidine pickup** | 350 DZD (deducted from refund) | 24–48 hours |
| **Algérie Télécom** | 200 DZD (deducted from refund) | 5–7 days |
| **DZShop Express (Algiers)** | Free | Same day |

> 💡 We recommend dropping the product at one of our stores for the fastest processing.

### Step 5: Refund Processing

After we receive and inspect the product, the refund is processed within **5–10 business days**. See the `refund_timeline.md` file in the `returns/` folder for details.

## Refund Methods

The refund is issued using the **same payment method** used for the original purchase:

| Original Payment | Refund Method | Time |
|-----------------|---------------|------|
| CIB card | Credit to same card | 5–7 business days |
| Edahabia | Credit to same card | 5–7 business days |
| BaridiMob | Credit to same account | 5–7 business days |
| DZShop Wallet | Credit to wallet | 24 hours |
| Cash on Delivery | Bank transfer or DZShop Wallet | 7–10 business days |
| Bank Transfer | Bank transfer | 7–10 business days |
| Installments | Refund to original payment schedule | 10–15 business days |

> ⚠️ **For COD orders**: We cannot refund in cash. The refund will be issued as a bank transfer or to the DZShop Wallet.

## Defective or Wrong Products

If you received a **defective product** or the **wrong item** (DZShop's mistake):

- The return shipping is **free** (we cover all costs).
- The refund is processed within **48 hours** of receiving the return.
- You can request a **replacement** instead of a refund.
- You may receive a **store credit** of 500 DZD as compensation for the inconvenience.

## Damaged in Transit

If the product arrives damaged:

1. **Refuse the delivery** if the damage is visible.
2. If you accepted the delivery, **take photos** of the damage immediately.
3. Contact us within **48 hours** at returns@dzshop.dz with:
   - Order number
   - Photos of the damaged product and packaging
   - Description of the damage
4. We will arrange a free pickup and send a replacement (or refund).

> ⚠️ **Important**: Claims for damage after 48 hours of delivery may not be accepted.

## Exchange Policy

Customers can exchange a product for:

- A different size (fashion items)
- A different color (if available)
- A different model (if the price difference is paid or refunded)

Exchanges follow the same process as returns. See the `exchange_policy.md` file in the `returns/` folder for details.

## Return Limits

| Customer Type | Returns per Month | Refund Amount Limit |
|----------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Standard customer** | 5 returns | 100,000 DZD |
| **VIP customer** (DZShop Points > 5,000) | 10 returns | 250,000 DZD |
| **B2B customer** | Per contract | Per contract |

> If a customer exceeds these limits, our team will contact them to discuss the situation. Excessive returns may result in account restrictions.

## Return Fraud

DZShop reserves the right to refuse returns in cases of suspected fraud, including:

- Returning a different product than the one purchased.
- Returning empty boxes.
- Returning counterfeit products.
- Repeated returns of the same product type.

Customers suspected of return fraud will be contacted, and their account may be suspended pending investigation.
