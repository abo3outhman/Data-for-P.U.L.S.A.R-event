## Delivery Time by Order Time

The time you place your order affects the delivery time:

| Order Time | Processing Starts | Same-Day Delivery (Algiers) |
|------------|------------------|------------------------------|
| Before 14:00 | Same day | ✅ Yes (within 4 hours) |
| After 14:00 | Next day (Sat–Thu) | Next day (within 4 hours) |
| Friday | Saturday | Saturday (within 4 hours) |
| Public holiday | Next business day | Next business day |

## Exceptions and Delays

Delivery times may be longer during:

1. **Ramadan**: +1–2 days for all shipping options.
2. **Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha**: +2–3 days (warehouses and shipping partners operate at reduced capacity).
3. **Public holidays**: +1 day per holiday.
4. **Black Friday (last Friday of November)**: +2–3 days (high order volume).
5. **Year-end holidays (December 25–January 2)**: +1–2 days.
6. **Weather events** (rare in Algeria, but possible in winter): +1–2 days.

## Delivery Time Guarantee

For DZShop Express (same-day delivery in Algiers):

- **Guarantee**: 4-hour delivery or your shipping fee is refunded.
- **Conditions**:
  - Order placed before 14:00.
  - Products in stock.
  - Delivery address in Algiers wilaya.
  - Weather conditions allow delivery.

For other shipping options, delivery times are **estimates**, not guarantees. We do our best to meet them, but delays can occur due to:
- Shipping partner issues.
- Weather conditions.
- Customs delays (no customs within Algeria, but security checkpoints in some areas).
- High order volume (sales, holidays).

## What to Do If Delivery Is Late

If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated delivery time:

1. **Track your order** (see `track_order.md`).
2. **Wait 24 hours** past the estimated date (in case of minor delays).
3. **Contact us** if still not delivered after 24 hours:
   - Hotline 3030
   - WhatsApp +213 770 12 34 56
   - Email: support@dzshop.dz
4. We'll investigate with the shipping partner and provide an updated estimate.
5. If the delay is significant (more than 7 days past estimate), we may offer a **store credit** of 500 DZD as compensation.
