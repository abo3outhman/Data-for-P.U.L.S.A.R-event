## Pricing Policy

- All prices are in **Algerian Dinar (DZD)**.
- Prices include **VAT (TVA) at 19%**.
- Prices do **not** include shipping fees (calculated at checkout).
- Prices may vary between online and in-store (in-store prices are usually lower).

## Product Availability

- **In Stock**: Product is available and will be shipped within 24 hours.
- **Low Stock**: Less than 5 units remaining.
- **Out of Stock**: Product is not available. Customers can subscribe to "notify me" alerts.
- **Pre-order**: Product will be available soon. Customers can pre-order with a 20% deposit.
