# DZShop — How to Order

## Overview

Placing an order on DZShop is simple. This guide walks you through the process step by step.

## Prerequisites

Before placing an order, you need:

1. **A DZShop account** (or you can check out as a guest — see below).
2. **A delivery address** in Algeria (one of the 58 wilayas).
3. **A payment method** (CIB, Edahabia, BaridiMob, COD, Bank Transfer, or DZShop Wallet).

## Step-by-Step Ordering Process

### Step 1: Browse Products

- Visit `dzshop.dz` or open the DZShop mobile app.
- Browse by category (Electronics, Fashion, Home, etc.).
- Use the **search bar** at the top to find specific products.
- Filter by price, brand, rating, or availability.

### Step 2: Add to Cart

- Click on a product to view its details.
- Select the desired options (size, color, quantity).
- Click **"Add to Cart"**.
- A small notification will appear confirming the item was added.
- You can continue shopping or proceed to checkout.

### Step 3: View Your Cart

- Click the **cart icon** (top-right corner).
- Review the items in your cart:
  - Product name
  - Quantity
  - Price
  - Subtotal
- You can:
  - **Change quantity** (using + and - buttons)
  - **Remove items** (click the trash icon)
  - **Save for later** (move item to a wishlist)
  - **Apply a coupon code** (if you have one)

### Step 4: Proceed to Checkout

- Click **"Proceed to Checkout"**.
- You will be asked to:
  - **Log in** to your DZShop account, OR
  - **Check out as a guest** (you'll need to provide an email and phone number)

> 💡 We recommend creating an account for:
> - Faster checkout next time
> - Order tracking
> - Loyalty points
> - Easier returns

### Step 5: Enter Delivery Address

- Fill in the delivery address form:
  - Full name
  - Phone number (must be valid Algerian number)
  - Wilaya (select from dropdown)
  - Commune (select from dropdown based on wilaya)
  - Address (street, building, apartment)
  - Delivery instructions (optional)
- You can save multiple addresses in your account.
- Select the address for this order.

### Step 6: Choose Shipping Option

Select your preferred shipping option:

| Option | Delivery Time | Cost |
|--------|---------------|------|
| DZShop Express (Algiers only) | 4 hours | 600 DZD |
| Express (24h) | 24 hours | Calculated |
| Standard (48–72h) | 2–3 days | Calculated |
| Economy (4–6 days) | 4–6 days | Calculated |
| Budget (5–10 days) | 5–10 days | Cheapest |
| Click & Collect | 4 hours | Free |

> ⚠️ Some shipping options may not be available depending on your wilaya or the products in your cart.

### Step 7: Choose Payment Method

Select your preferred payment method:

- CIB card
- Edahabia card
- BaridiMob
- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Bank Transfer
- DZShop Wallet
- Installment Plan (3x, 6x, or 12x)

### Step 8: Review Your Order

- Review all details:
  - Products and quantities
  - Delivery address
  - Shipping option
  - Payment method
  - Total amount (including shipping and any fees)
- Read and accept the **Terms and Conditions**.
- Click **"Place Order"**.

### Step 9: Payment

- If you chose **online payment** (CIB, Edahabia, BaridiMob, Wallet):
  - You'll be redirected to the secure payment page.
  - Enter your card details or authentication code.
  - Wait for the payment confirmation.
- If you chose **COD**:
  - No payment is needed now. Pay when the order is delivered.
- If you chose **Bank Transfer**:
  - You'll receive an email with DZShop's bank details.
  - Make the transfer within 48 hours and send proof to payments@dzshop.dz.
- If you chose **Installments**:
  - The first installment is charged immediately.
  - Subsequent installments are charged on the same date each month.

### Step 10: Order Confirmation

After successful order placement, you will receive:

1. **On-screen confirmation** with your order number.
2. **Email confirmation** (within 5 minutes).
3. **SMS confirmation** (within 10 minutes).
4. **In-app notification** (if you have the app).

The confirmation includes:
- Order number (e.g., DZ-2025-0012345)
- List of products
- Delivery address
- Shipping option and expected delivery date
- Payment details
- Tracking link (active once the order is shipped)

## Order Number Format

DZShop order numbers follow this format:

```
DZ-YYYY-NNNNNNN
```

Where:
- `DZ` — Fixed prefix
- `YYYY` — Year (e.g., 2025)
- `NNNNNNN` — Sequential number

**Example:** `DZ-2025-0012345`

> 💡 Keep your order number safe — you'll need it for tracking, returns, and customer support.

## Common Issues

### "My payment was declined"

- Check that your card details are correct.
- Ensure you have sufficient funds.
- Contact your bank (they may have blocked the transaction).
- Try a different payment method.
- If the issue persists, contact `payments@dzshop.dz`.

### "I can't complete my order"

- Check your internet connection.
- Try a different browser or device.
- Clear your browser cache.
- Contact support at hotline 3030 if the issue persists.

### "The product shows 'Out of Stock' after I added it to cart"

- Products in your cart are not reserved.
- If a product goes out of stock before checkout, you'll see a notification.
- You can remove the out-of-stock item and continue, or wait for it to be back in stock (use the "Notify Me" feature).
