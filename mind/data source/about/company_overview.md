# DZShop — Company Overview

## About Us

**DZShop** is a leading Algerian e-commerce platform founded in **2020** by a group of young Algerian entrepreneurs. We are headquartered in **Algiers** and operate across all 58 wilayas of Algeria.

Our mission is to make online shopping simple, accessible, and trustworthy for every Algerian citizen — from the big cities to the most remote areas of the Sahara.

## Our Story

DZShop started in 2020 with a simple idea: Algerians deserve a reliable, local e-commerce experience. At the time, most online shopping in Algeria was done through foreign platforms with high shipping costs and long delivery times.

Our founders — Karim Belkacem (CEO), Amine Saadi (CTO), and Yasmine Bouzid (COO) — started with just 3 employees and a small warehouse in Bab Ezzouar. Today, we have:

- 5 physical stores across 16 wilayas
- 87 employees
- More than 250,000 active customers
- Over 12,000 products in our catalog
- An average of 1,500 orders per day

## Our Values

1. **Trust** — We build long-term relationships with our customers through honesty and transparency.
2. **Local First** — We support Algerian producers and brands whenever possible.
3. **Accessibility** — Our platform works on low-end devices and slow internet connections.
4. **Customer Service** — Every customer deserves a respectful, helpful response.
