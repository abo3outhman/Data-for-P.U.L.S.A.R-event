# DZShop — Modify or Cancel an Order

## Overview

Orders on DZShop can be **modified** or **cancelled** under certain conditions. This guide explains when and how.

## Time Limits

| Action | Time Limit | Condition |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| **Modify** order | Within **2 hours** of placement | Order not yet shipped |
| **Cancel** order | Before order is **shipped** | Order status: "Placed" or "Confirmed" |
| **Cancel after shipment** | ❌ Not possible | Order must be returned after delivery |

> ⚠️ **Important:** Once an order is shipped, it cannot be cancelled. The customer must wait for delivery and then initiate a return.

## Modifying an Order

### What Can Be Modified?

Within 2 hours of placing the order, you can modify:

| Modification | Allowed? |
|--------------|----------|
| Change delivery address | ✅ Yes (same wilaya only) |
| Change shipping option | ✅ Yes |
| Change payment method | ✅ Yes (if not yet processed) |
| Add items to the order | ✅ Yes |
| Remove items from the order | ✅ Yes |
| Change product size/color | ✅ Yes |
| Change delivery instructions | ✅ Yes |
| Change to a different wilaya | ❌ No (must cancel and reorder) |
| Apply a coupon code | ✅ Yes (if not already applied) |
| Remove a coupon code | ❌ No (once applied, it stays) |

### How to Modify an Order

#### Method 1: Website/App

1. Log in to your DZShop account.
2. Go to **"My Orders"**.
3. Select the order you want to modify.
4. Click **"Modify Order"** (only visible if within 2 hours and order is not shipped).
5. Make your changes.
6. Click **"Save Changes"**.
7. You'll receive a confirmation email with the updated order details.

#### Method 2: Phone

1. Call our hotline **3030**.
2. Press **2** for order modifications.
3. Provide your order number and identity verification.
4. Tell the agent what you want to modify.
5. The agent will make the changes and send a confirmation.

#### Method 3: WhatsApp

1. Send a message to **+213 770 12 34 56**.
2. Send your order number.
3. Specify what you want to modify.
4. A customer service agent will assist you (during working hours).

#### Method 4: Email

1. Email **orders@dzshop.dz** with:
   - Your order number in the subject line.
   - The modifications you want.
   - Your phone number for verification.
2. Our team will respond within **2 hours** (during working hours).

> ⚠️ Email modifications may take longer to process. We recommend using the website/app or phone for urgent modifications.

### After Modification

- If the order total **increases**, you'll be charged the difference.
- If the order total **decreases**, the difference will be refunded (5–10 business days).
- If you change the shipping option, the delivery time may change.
- You'll receive an updated order confirmation email.

## Cancelling an Order

### When Can You Cancel?

You can cancel an order if its status is:

- ✅ **Order Placed** — Within 2 hours of placement.
- ✅ **Order Confirmed** — Before it's shipped.

You **cannot** cancel if the status is:

- ❌ **Order Shipped** — Must wait for delivery, then return.
- ❌ **Out for Delivery** — Too late.
- ❌ **Delivered** — Must initiate a return.

### How to Cancel an Order

#### Method 1: Website/App

1. Log in to your DZShop account.
2. Go to **"My Orders"**.
3. Select the order you want to cancel.
4. Click **"Cancel Order"**.
5. Select a cancellation reason (from dropdown).
6. Click **"Confirm Cancellation"**.
7. You'll receive a cancellation confirmation email.

#### Method 2: Phone

1. Call our hotline **3030**.
2. Press **3** for cancellations.
3. Provide your order number and identity verification.
4. Confirm the cancellation.

#### Method 3: WhatsApp

1. Send a message to **+213 770 12 34 56**.
2. Send your order number.
3. Specify that you want to cancel the order.
4. Provide a reason (optional).

#### Method 4: Email

1. Email **orders@dzshop.dz** with:
   - "CANCEL" + order number in the subject line.
   - Your phone number for verification.
2. Our team will process the cancellation within 2 hours.

### Cancellation Reasons

When cancelling, you'll be asked to select a reason:

1. Changed my mind
2. Found a better price elsewhere
3. Ordered by mistake
4. Delivery time too long
5. Payment issue
6. Want to reorder with different items
7. Other (please specify)

This helps us improve our service.

### After Cancellation

- The order status changes to **"Cancelled"**.
- You'll receive a confirmation email within 5 minutes.
- **Refunds** are processed based on the original payment method:

| Original Payment | Refund Time |
|-----------------|-------------|
| CIB card | 5–7 business days |
| Edahabia | 5–7 business days |
| BaridiMob | 5–7 business days |
| DZShop Wallet | 24 hours |
| Cash on Delivery | No refund needed (you didn't pay yet) |
| Bank Transfer | 7–10 business days |
| Installments | 10–15 business days (first installment refunded) |

## Partial Cancellation

You cannot partially cancel an order (e.g., cancel one item but keep the others). To remove specific items:

1. **Cancel** the entire order.
2. **Place a new order** with only the items you want.

## Cancelling Installment Orders

For orders paid with installments:

- If cancelled before the first payment: full cancellation, no charges.
- If cancelled after the first payment: refund of the first installment (10–15 business days).
- Future installments are cancelled automatically.

## What if the Order is Already Being Prepared?

If the order status is **"Confirmed"** (being prepared), our team will try to stop the preparation. However:

- If the order has already been packed, we may not be able to cancel it.
- In this case, you'll need to refuse the delivery or return the package after delivery.
- Refund will be processed after we receive the returned package.

## Cancelled by DZShop

In rare cases, DZShop may cancel an order. Reasons include:

- Product out of stock (we'll notify you within 24 hours).
- Payment verification failed.
- Suspicious activity detected.
- Delivery address in an unserviced area.
- Pricing error on the website.

In all cases:
- You'll receive an email explaining the cancellation.
- A full refund is processed immediately.
- If you used a coupon, it's returned to your account.
