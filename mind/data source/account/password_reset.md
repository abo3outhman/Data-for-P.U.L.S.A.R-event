# DZShop — Password Reset

## Overview

Forgot your DZShop password? This guide walks you through resetting it.

## How to Reset Your Password

### Step 1: Go to the Reset Page

1. Go to `dzshop.dz/login`.
2. Click **"Forgot Password?"** (below the login form).
3. You'll be redirected to `dzshop.dz/reset-password`.

### Step 2: Enter Your Email or Phone

1. Enter the **email address** or **phone number** associated with your account.
2. Complete the CAPTCHA (to prevent automated attacks).
3. Click **"Send Reset Link"**.

### Step 3: Receive the Reset Link

You'll receive:

- **Email**: A password reset link (valid for 30 minutes).
- **SMS** (if you entered a phone number): A 6-digit code (valid for 10 minutes).

### Step 4: Click the Reset Link

1. Open the email from DZShop.
2. Click the **"Reset Password"** button.
3. You'll be redirected to a secure page.

> ⚠️ If you don't see the email:
> - Check your spam/junk folder.
> - Wait 10 minutes.
> - Make sure you entered the correct email.
> - Request a new link.

### Step 5: Set a New Password

1. Enter your **new password**.
2. Confirm your **new password**.
3. Password requirements:
   - Minimum 8 characters.
   - At least 1 uppercase letter.
   - At least 1 lowercase letter.
   - At least 1 number.
   - Cannot be the same as your last 3 passwords.
4. Click **"Reset Password"**.

### Step 6: Confirmation

- Your password is now updated.
- You'll receive a confirmation email.
- You can log in with your new password.

## Reset via SMS (Phone Number)

If you registered with a phone number:

1. Go to `dzshop.dz/reset-password`.
2. Enter your phone number.
3. Click **"Send Code"**.
4. You'll receive an SMS with a 6-digit code.
5. Enter the code on the website.
6. Set a new password.
7. Click **"Reset Password"**.

## Reset via Hotline (Phone Call)

1. Call **3030**.
2. Press **6** for password reset.
3. Provide your account email or phone number.
4. Verify your identity (the agent may ask security questions).
5. The agent will send a reset link to your email.
6. Follow the email link to reset your password.

> ⚠️ Our agents **cannot** set a new password for you. They can only send a reset link.

## Reset via WhatsApp

1. Send a message to **+213 770 12 34 56**.
2. Send: `RESET PASSWORD [your email or phone]`.
3. A customer service agent will assist you (during working hours).
4. You'll receive a reset link via email.

## Password Reset Limits

To prevent abuse:

- Maximum **3 reset requests per hour** per email/phone.
- Maximum **5 reset requests per day** per email/phone.
- Reset links expire after **30 minutes** (email) or **10 minutes** (SMS).

## If You No Longer Have Access to Your Email

If you lost access to the email associated with your account:

1. Email `security@dzshop.dz` from a new email address.
2. Subject: "Account Recovery — Lost Email".
3. Provide:
   - Your account email (the one you lost).
   - Your phone number.
   - Your full name.
   - Your last order number (if you remember).
   - Your wilaya.
   - A copy of your ID (to verify identity).
4. Our team will review within **48 hours**.
5. If approved, we'll update your account with your new email.

## If You No Longer Have Access to Your Phone

If you lost your phone number:

1. Log in with your email and password (if you remember).
2. Go to **My Account** → **Profile** → **Phone Number**.
3. Click **"Change Phone Number"**.
4. Verify via email.
5. Enter your new phone number.
6. Verify via SMS to the new number.

If you can't log in:

1. Email `security@dzshop.dz`.
2. Subject: "Account Recovery — Lost Phone".
3. Provide:
   - Your account email.
   - Your old phone number.
   - Your full name.
   - Your ID copy.
4. Our team will review within 48 hours.

## Tips for a Strong Password

✅ **DO:**
- Use at least 12 characters.
- Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.
- Use a unique password (not used elsewhere).
- Use a password manager (Bitwarden, 1Password, KeePass).
- Change your password every 6 months.

❌ **DON'T:**
- Don't use personal info (name, birthdate, phone).
- Don't use common passwords (123456, password, qwerty).
- Don't reuse passwords from other sites.
- Don't share your password with anyone (including DZShop staff).

## After Resetting Your Password

For security:

- You'll be **logged out from all devices**.
- You'll need to log in again on each device.
- If 2FA is enabled, you'll need to verify again.
- Your recent orders and account info remain intact.

## Suspicious Activity

If you received a password reset email but **didn't request it**:

1. **Don't click the link**.
2. Someone may be trying to access your account.
3. Log in to your account (with your current password).
4. Change your password immediately.
5. Enable 2FA if not already enabled.
6. Email `security@dzshop.dz` to report the suspicious activity.
