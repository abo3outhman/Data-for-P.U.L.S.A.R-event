# DZShop — Delivery Fees

## Overview

This document lists the delivery fees for all shipping options across all 58 wilayas of Algeria.

## Shipping Cost Factors

The shipping cost depends on:

1. **Weight** of the package (in kg).
2. **Destination wilaya**.
3. **Shipping option** (Express, Standard, Economy, Budget).
4. **Delivery type** (Stop Desk vs. Home Delivery).

## Weight Tiers

| Tier | Weight Range | Notes |
|------|--------------|-------|
| **Light** | 0 – 1 kg | Small items (phone cases, books) |
| **Medium** | 1 – 5 kg | Most products (clothing, electronics) |
| **Heavy** | 5 – 15 kg | Large items (TVs, small appliances) |
| **Very Heavy** | 15 – 30 kg | Furniture, large appliances |
| **Oversized** | 30+ kg | Special pricing (contact us) |

## DZShop Express (Same-Day, Algiers Only)

## Additional Fees

### Fragile Item Surcharge
- **+200 DZD** for products marked as fragile (glass, TVs, monitors).

### Large Item Surcharge
- **+500 DZD** for items exceeding 30 kg or 1.5m in any dimension.

### COD Handling Fee
- **+200 DZD** for Cash on Delivery orders (in addition to shipping fee).

### Remote Area Surcharge
- **+300 DZD** for deliveries to remote areas (more than 50 km from the wilaya capital).

### Saturday Delivery
- **+200 DZD** for Saturday delivery (only available in major cities).

### Hold at Stop Desk (Extended)
- Free for 7 days.
- **+50 DZD per day** after 7 days (up to 14 days, then returned to sender).

## Shipping Fee Calculator

Customers can calculate the exact shipping fee before placing an order:

1. Add products to cart.
2. Go to checkout.
3. Enter delivery address.
4. The system calculates the fee automatically based on:
   - Total weight of the order.
   - Destination wilaya.
   - Available shipping options.

### Example 1: Smartphone to Algiers
- Product: Smartphone (0.5 kg)
- Destination: Algiers, Hydra
