## Pickup Points Network

In wilayas where we don't have a physical store, we partner with local businesses (pharmacies, supermarkets, telecom shops) to serve as **pickup points**. Customers can collect their orders from these points after receiving an SMS notification.

To find the nearest pickup point, customers can:
1. Visit our website: `dzshop.dz/pickup-points`
2. Use the DZShop mobile app
3. Call our hotline: **3030**

## Click & Collect Service

Customers who prefer to order online and pick up in-store can use the **Click & Collect** option at checkout. Benefits:

- **Free pickup** — No delivery fees
- **Ready in 4 hours** — For orders placed before 14:00
- **Ready next day** — For orders placed after 14:00
- **7 days to collect** — After 7 days, the order is cancelled and refunded

## Store Services

All our physical stores offer:

- **Product pickup** (Click & Collect)
- **Returns and exchanges** (within 7 days)
- **Customer service** (in Arabic, French, and Tamazight)
- **Product demonstrations** (electronics only)
- **Cash payment** (for orders placed online with "Pay in Store" option)

## Store Locator

Customers can locate the nearest store or pickup point by:
- Entering their wilaya code on the website
- Using GPS on the mobile app
- Calling our hotline **3030**
