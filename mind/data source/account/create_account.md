# DZShop — Create an Account

## Overview

Creating a DZShop account is **free** and gives you access to:
- Faster checkout
- Order tracking
- Loyalty points (DZShop Points)
- Wishlist
- Multiple saved addresses
- Order history
- Exclusive offers

## How to Create an Account

### Method 1: Website

1. Go to `dzshop.dz`.
2. Click **"Sign Up"** (top-right corner).
3. Fill in the registration form:
   - **First name**
   - **Last name**
   - **Email address** (must be valid)
   - **Phone number** (Algerian format: 0X XX XX XX XX)
   - **Password** (at least 8 characters, 1 uppercase, 1 number)
   - **Confirm password**
4. Read and accept the **Terms and Conditions**.
5. Check the box to receive promotional emails (optional).
6. Click **"Create Account"**.
7. You'll receive a verification email.
8. Click the verification link in the email.
9. Your account is now active.

### Method 2: Mobile App

1. Download the **DZShop app** (Android or iOS).
2. Open the app.
3. Tap **"Sign Up"**.
4. Fill in the same form as the website.
5. Tap **"Create Account"**.
6. Verify via the email link.

### Method 3: Phone (Hotline)

1. Call **3030**.
2. Press **5** for account creation.
3. Provide your details to the agent.
4. The agent will create the account and send a verification email.
5. Verify via the email link.

> ⚠️ Phone-created accounts still require email verification before the first order.

## Account Information

### Required Fields

- First name
- Last name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Password

### Optional Fields

- Date of birth (for birthday discounts)
- Wilaya (for local offers)
- Gender (for personalized recommendations)

## Account Verification

After creating your account:

1. **Email verification** (required):
   - Click the link in the verification email.
   - Must be done within 24 hours (or the account is deleted).
2. **Phone verification** (required for first order):
   - You'll receive an SMS with a 6-digit code.
   - Enter the code on the website or app.
   - Must be done before placing your first order.

## Account Security

### Password Requirements

- Minimum 8 characters.
- At least 1 uppercase letter.
- At least 1 lowercase letter.
- At least 1 number.
- Special characters are recommended (but not required).

### Password Recommendations

- Use a unique password (don't reuse from other sites).
- Don't use personal information (name, birthdate, phone).
- Use a password manager.
- Change your password every 6 months.

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

DZShop supports 2FA for additional security:

1. Go to **My Account** → **Security**.
2. Click **"Enable 2FA"**.
3. Choose method: SMS or Authenticator App.
4. Follow the setup instructions.
5. 2FA is required for:
   - Login from a new device.
   - Changing account information.
   - Orders above 50,000 DZD.

## Account Types

### Standard Customer (Free)
- Default account type.
- All basic features.
- Loyalty points.

### VIP Customer (DZShop Points > 5,000)
- Earned by accumulating 5,000+ DZShop Points.
- Benefits:
  - 5% discount on all orders.
  - Free Express shipping in Algiers.
  - Priority customer service.
  - Early access to sales.
  - Higher return limits (10/month, up to 250,000 DZD).

### B2B Customer (Business)
- For businesses buying in bulk.
- Apply at `dzshop.dz/b2b`.
- Benefits:
  - Special pricing.
  - Dedicated account manager.
  - Invoice billing.
  - Bulk shipping rates.

## Common Issues

### "I didn't receive the verification email"

- Check your spam/junk folder.
- Make sure you entered the correct email.
- Wait 10 minutes (sometimes emails are delayed).
- Request a new verification email from the login page.
- If still no email, contact support@dzshop.dz.

### "I didn't receive the SMS code"

- Make sure your phone number is correct.
- Check your signal.
- Wait 5 minutes.
- Request a new code (max 3 attempts per hour).
- If still no SMS, contact us.

### "My account is locked"

After 5 failed login attempts, your account is locked for **30 minutes**.

To unlock:
- Wait 30 minutes, OR
- Use the "Forgot Password" feature, OR
- Contact support.

### "I forgot my password"

See the `password_reset.md` file in this folder.

## Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time:

1. Go to **My Account** → **Settings** → **Delete Account**.
2. Confirm your password.
3. Select a reason for deletion.
4. Click **"Delete My Account"**.

> ⚠️ **Account deletion is permanent.** All your data, order history, and loyalty points will be lost. Pending orders and refunds will still be processed, but you won't be able to access them.

By Algerian law (Law 18-07 on Personal Data Protection), we retain some transactional data for 5 years after account deletion for legal and tax purposes.
