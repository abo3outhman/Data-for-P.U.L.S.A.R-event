# DZShop — Refund Process

## Overview

This document explains how refunds work at DZShop — when you're eligible, how to request one, and how long it takes.

## When Are You Eligible for a Refund?

You can request a refund in these situations:

### 1. Returned Product
- You returned a product within the 7-day return window.
- The product is in original condition.
- See `returns/return_policy.md` for return conditions.

### 2. Cancelled Order
- You cancelled the order before it was shipped.
- See `orders/modify_order.md` for cancellation process.

### 3. Defective Product
- You received a defective product.
- DZShop's fault (not customer misuse).

### 4. Wrong Product Received
- We shipped the wrong item.
- Refund is processed within 48 hours.

### 5. Order Not Delivered
- The order was lost in transit.
- Confirmed missing after 30 days.

### 6. Duplicate Charge
- You were charged twice for the same order.
- We refund the duplicate amount within 48 hours.

### 7. Pricing Error
- We charged you more than the listed price.
- We refund the difference within 24 hours.

## Refund Methods

The refund is issued using the **same payment method** used for the original purchase:

| Original Payment | Refund Method | Processing Time |
|-----------------|---------------|-----------------|
| CIB card | Credit to same card | 5–7 business days |
| Edahabia | Credit to same card | 5–7 business days |
| BaridiMob | Credit to same account | 5–7 business days |
| DZShop Wallet | Credit to wallet | 24 hours |
| Cash on Delivery | Bank transfer or DZShop Wallet | 7–10 business days |
| Bank Transfer | Bank transfer | 7–10 business days |
| Installments | Refund to original schedule | 10–15 business days |

> ⚠️ **For COD orders**: We cannot refund in cash. The refund will be issued as a bank transfer (requires your RIB) or to your DZShop Wallet.

## How to Request a Refund

### Method 1: Website/App (For Returns)

1. Log in to your DZShop account.
2. Go to **"My Orders"**.
3. Select the order with the product to return.
4. Click **"Return Item"**.
5. Select the reason for return.
6. Choose refund method (auto-selected based on original payment).
7. Submit the request.

### Method 2: Email

1. Email `returns@dzshop.dz` (for returns) or `payments@dzshop.dz` (for payment issues).
2. Subject: "Refund Request — [Order Number]".
3. Body should include:
   - Order number
   - Reason for refund
   - Photos (if defective or wrong product)
   - Your bank details (RIB) if refund is by bank transfer
4. Our team will respond within 24 hours.

### Method 3: Hotline

1. Call **3030**.
2. Press **3** for refunds.
3. Provide your order number and reason.
4. The agent will initiate the refund request.

### Method 4: WhatsApp

1. Send a message to **+213 770 12 34 56**.
2. Send: "REFUND [order number] [reason]".
3. A customer service agent will assist you.

### Method 5: In-Store

1. Visit any of our 5 physical stores.
2. Bring the product (if returning), original receipt, and ID.
3. The store staff will process the refund.

## Refund Process Timeline

### Step 1: Request Submitted
- Customer initiates the refund request.
- Acknowledgment email sent within **5 minutes**.

### Step 2: Review
- Our team reviews the request.
- For returns: We wait to receive the returned product.
- For defective/wrong products: We may request photos or videos.
- **Time**: 24–48 hours (or until product is received for returns).

### Step 3: Approval
- If approved, the refund is initiated.
- Approval email sent with refund details.
- **Time**: Within 24 hours of review completion.

### Step 4: Processing
- The refund is sent to the payment processor.
- Processing time depends on the payment method (see table above).

### Step 5: Refund Received
- Customer sees the refund in their account/card.
- Confirmation email sent.

## Refund Amount

The refund amount includes:

- ✅ Product price
- ✅ Original shipping fee (proportional to returned items)
- ❌ Return shipping fee (deducted if DZShop pays for return shipping)
- ❌ COD handling fee (200 DZD, non-refundable)
- ❌ Installment processing fees (if applicable)

### Partial Refunds

If you return some items from an order (not all):

- The refund covers only the returned items.
- Original shipping fee is partially refunded (proportional).
- The COD handling fee (200 DZD) is not refunded.

## Refund for Installment Orders

If you paid with installments and want a refund:

1. **Order cancelled before first payment**:
   - No charges. Order cancelled.

2. **Order cancelled after first payment, before second**:
   - First installment is refunded (10–15 business days).
   - Future installments are cancelled.

3. **Order cancelled after multiple installments**:
   - All paid installments are refunded.
   - Future installments are cancelled.
   - Refund processing time: 10–15 business days.

4. **Product returned after all installments paid**:
   - Full refund of all installments.
   - Processing time: 10–15 business days.

## Refund Tracking

You can track your refund status:

1. **Website**: My Account → My Orders → Refund Status.
2. **App**: My Orders → Select Order → Refund Details.
3. **Email**: Refund confirmation email contains a tracking link.
4. **Hotline**: 3030, option 3.

### Refund Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Requested** | Refund requested, awaiting review |
| **Approved** | Refund approved, awaiting processing |
| **Processing** | Refund sent to payment processor |
| **Completed** | Refund received by customer |
| **Rejected** | Refund denied (with reason) |

## Refund Not Received?

If you haven't received your refund within the expected timeframe:

1. **Check your bank statement** (refunds may appear as "DZShop Refund" or "SATIM Refund").
2. **Verify the refund method** (was it to your card, wallet, or bank account?).
3. **Wait 3 extra business days** (banks sometimes delay refunds).
4. **Contact us** with your refund tracking ID:
   - Email: payments@dzshop.dz
   - Hotline: 3030
   - WhatsApp: +213 770 12 34 56

We will investigate and respond within **48 hours**.

## Refund Disputes

If you disagree with our refund decision:

1. Email `disputes@dzshop.dz` within **7 days** of the decision.
2. Provide additional evidence (photos, communications, etc.).
3. Our escalation team will review within **5 business days**.
4. Final decision is communicated by email.
